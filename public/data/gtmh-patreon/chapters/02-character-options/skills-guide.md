# Skills and Hunt Roles

Source section: `Your Role on a Hunt` from `public/data/gtmh-patreon/gtmh-patreon.md`.

## Trailblazer
Primary skill: Survival. Leads route decisions and finding signs checks.

## Spotter
Primary skill: Investigation. Finds resources in area-specific checks.

## Scout
Primary skills: Perception and Stealth. Identifies threats and hazards in new areas.

## Artisan
Primary skills: Cooking utensils and selected ability checks. Handles meal prep and non-combat preparation devices.
