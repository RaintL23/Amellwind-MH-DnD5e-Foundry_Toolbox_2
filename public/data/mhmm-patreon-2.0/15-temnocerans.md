## Family description

Temnoceran are a class of monster characterized by its arachnoid traits, such as the ability to produce silk. Most species possess six limbs, though eight-limbed forms like the rakna-kadaki also occur. They share a compact cephalothorax, a segmented abdomen, and a sturdy chitinous exoskeleton, and are primarily predatory. Their silk is used for nests, egg cases, and restraining prey.
