## Family description

Carapaceon are crustacean-like monsters that have hard shells and exoskeletons or crab-like bodies, When they're weakened by physical damage, they show internal bleeding by the frothing of the purple bubbles from their mouths. Carapaceon are known to hide underground and attack their prey once within range. Most of these crustaceans resemble crabs, lobsters or scorpions.
