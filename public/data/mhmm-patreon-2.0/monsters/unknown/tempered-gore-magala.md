## Traits

### Frenzy

When a creature gains its third frenzy charge, it must make a DC 22 Constitution saving throw or be diseased with the frenzy virus until dispelled by a greater restoration or wish spell on a failed save, or isn't diseased and its frenzy charges reset to zero on a successful one.

### Legendary Resistance (3/Day)

If the gore magala fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.


## Actions

### Multiattack

The gore magala makes one Bite attack and one Tail attack. It can use Frenzy Pool after its Tail attack.

### Bite

Melee Weapon Attack: +15 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 21 (3d8 + 8) piercing damage, and the target gains one frenzy charge.

### Body Slam

Melee Weapon Attack: +15 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 24 (3d10 + 8) bludgeoning damage. If the gore magala moved at least 10 feet straight toward the target immediately before the hit, the target takes an extra 16 (3d10) bludgeoning damage. If the target is a creature, it must succeed on a DC 23 Strength saving throw or be pushed up to 10 feet away and knocked prone.

### Frenzy Burst

Ranged Weapon Attack: +11 to hit, range 80/320 ft., one target. Hit: 17 (5d6) necrotic damage, and the target gains 1 frenzy charge. If the gore magala's frenzied state has activated in the last hour, it instead creates multiple frenzy explosions in a 25-foot line that is 5 feet wide centered on the target and perpendicular to the gore magala. Each creature in that line, takes 24 (7d6) necrotic damage and gain one frenzy charge.

### Tail

Melee Weapon Attack: +15 to hit, reach 15 ft., one target. Hit: 21 (3d10 + 8) bludgeoning damage.

### Winged Arm (Frenzied State Only)

Melee Weapon Attack: +15 to hit, reach 15 ft., one target. Hit: 15 (2d6 + 8) bludgeoning damage.

### Frenzy Breath (Recharge 5-6)

The gore magala exhales projectiles of concentrated frenzy virus in a 90-foot cone. Each creature in that area must make a DC 22 Dexterity saving throw, taking 63 (14d8) necrotic damage and gain 1 frenzy charge on a failed save. On a successful save, a target takes half as much damage and does not gain a frenzy charge. If the gore magala's frenzied state has activated in the last hour, it deals an extra 18 (4d8) necrotic damage and a creature gains two frenzy charges on a failed save.

### Bonus Action Frenzy Pool

Immediately after the gore magala makes a tail attack, a pool of purple mist fills the area in a 5-foot radius underneath the gore magala until the start of its next turn. A creature that enters the mist for the first time on a turn or ends its turn in the mist takes 17 (5d6) necrotic damage and gains 1 frenzy charge.


## Legendary Actions

The gore magala can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action option can be used at a time and only at the end of another creature's turn. The gore magala regains spent legendary actions at the start of its turn.

### Detect

The gore magala makes a Wisdom (Perception) check.

### Viral Discharge

Ranged Weapon Attack: +11 to hit, range 80/320 ft., one target. Hit: 17 (5d6) necrotic damage or 24 (7d6) if the gore magala's Frenzied State has activated in the last hour, and the target gains 1 frenzy charge.

### Close Range Frenzy Burst (Costs 2 Actions)

The gore magala releases three explosions of the frenzy virus in a 5-foot line that is 15 feet wide centered on a point in front of it. Each creature in that area must make a DC 22 Dexterity saving throw, taking 17 (5d6) necrotic damage and gain one frenzy charge on a failed save, or half as much damage and doesn't gain a frenzy charge on a successful one. If the gore magala's frenzied state has activated in the last hour, it instead creates five explosions in a 5-foot line that is 25-feet wide centered on a point in front of it.


## Mythic Actions

If the gore magala's Frenzied State trait has activated in the last hour, it can use the options below as legendary actions.

### Winged Arms

The gore magala makes two Winged Arm attacks.

### Ground Slam (Costs 2 Actions)

The gore magala rises up and slams its winged arms down on the ground beneath it. Each creature in a 15-foot square originating from the gore magala must make a DC 23 Dexterity saving throw, taking 30 (4d10 + 8) bludgeoning damage and be pushed up to 10 feet away and knocked prone on a failed save, or half as much damage, isn't pushed away, or knocked prone on a successful one. 575
