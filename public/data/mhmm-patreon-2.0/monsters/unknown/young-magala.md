# Young Magala

## Bio

The Young magala looks similar to its adult counterpart, but its body is smooth and it has not yet developed its wings or second set of arms. Though it is still able to spew its viral discharge at creatures, it is unable to spread the frenzy virus through its mouth and claws. Once it emerges from its host, the magala searches for a place to hide.

This may end up being the sewers in a city, perhaps a cave, or high in the mountains. Wherever it goes, the young magala is extremely territorial, attacking any and all creatures that enter its lair.

## Traits

### Death Burst

The young magala explodes when it drops to 0 hit points. Each creature within 20 feet of it must succeed on a DC 15 Constitution saving throw or take 10 (3d6) necrotic damage and become infected with the frenzy virus and a magala parasite on a failed save.
