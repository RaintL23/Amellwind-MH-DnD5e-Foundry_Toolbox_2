# Bnahabra

## Bio

Bnahabra are similar to the Vespoid. Bnahabra fly in a similar fashion, but there are various subspecies of the Bnahabra which vary between the regions, each with distinctly colored wings. Bnahabra are often seen attached to the sides of the walls seemingly lifeless, until an adventurer is close enough then they fly away or attack.

Unlike the Vespoid, Bnahabra can shoot a corrosive liquid which will reduce an adventurer's elemental resistance.
