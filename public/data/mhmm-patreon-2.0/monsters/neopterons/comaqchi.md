# Comaqchi

## Bio

The comaqchi is a small neopteron with a squat armored body, striking forelegs, and a tail tipped with a stinger that delivers a sleep-inducing agent. It forms dense colonies that cling to cliff faces and cavern walls in cold, windswept tundra, where individuals stay largely stationary until prey or intruders pass within reach.

When roused, comaqchi lash out with rapid foreleg strikes and sting to incapacitate, then swarm sluggish targets.
