# Desert Seltas

## Bio

Unlike Seltas, Desert Seltas have a dualpronged canopy rather than a single spike. It uses it to burrow and spend long periods underground, to move through compacted sand and stone. The canopy’s points can pierce rock and pry or haul loose debris from tunnels. A thin lubricating film secreted over the body reduces friction and shields the cuticle while digging.

An internal gland produces a paralytic venom that circulates through the body and can be expelled from the abdomen to arrest prey. Like its close relative, it retains functional wings and can launch into brief high-speed flights between burrow sites.
