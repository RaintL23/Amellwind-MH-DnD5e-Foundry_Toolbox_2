# Desert Seltas Queen

## Bio

The desert seltas queen is a very large neopteron, a subspecies of the seltas queen and the female counterpart of the desert seltas. Desert seltas queens are large, highly opportunistic predators with a high place in the food chain. Herbivores are common prey, but other monsters may also be taken; even a desert seltas may be eaten.

Against competitors they rely on an incredibly tough exoskeleton, powerful grasping forearms, mucus projectiles, and extremely long, deadly tail pincers. They are a force to be reckoned with. To fit the dune environment, the desert seltas queen has evolved a thick, sand-colored shell for camouflage.

Like the seltas queen, desert seltas queens bear large tail pincers that are longer than those of other species. They use these to pull sleeping desert seltas from the sand and force them to fight alongside them. During extraction the queen injects a pheromone-like substance that ends resistance and places the male under complete control, doing whatever she requires.

In battle the male and female fight as one, making the already dangerous seltas queen even deadlier as the two neopterons act in concert. The desert seltas queen can also spit high-pressure watery mucus from her mandibles to slow and injure prey, as the seltas queen does. She further employs an attack known to the Guild as the “beetle cannon,” using compressed mucus to launch a desert seltas at great speed as a living projectile, killing the male on impact.

Afterward the queen searches by scent and digs to find a replacement desert seltas, a practice that earned her the title “Cannon Beetle.”

## Bonus Actions

### Desert Drone Slave

The seltas queen stabs her tail into the ground, pulling out a burrowed desert seltas. The desert seltas is under the seltas queen's complete control, acts on her turn, and remains mounted atop the seltas queen until knocked off, the seltas queen falls prone, or is it used in the seltas queen's super cannon attack. Once used, the seltas queen can't use this trait again until the desert seltas dies, or it is more than 120 feet away from the seltas queen when she starts her turn.
