# Hornetaur

## Bio

Hornetaurs sport a large horn for their size that may be used for defense. Their legs are strong, much like a Grasshopper's, so they can jump great distances. Their wings are sharp enough to cut human skin just by brushing against them. Hornetaurs will frequently attack the adventurer in groups, making it irksome to fight other monsters in the area.

Hornetaur are ravenous eaters that can eat away whole fields in a day's time. They will pretty much eat everything in an area like a locust, causing major amounts of damage while doing so.
