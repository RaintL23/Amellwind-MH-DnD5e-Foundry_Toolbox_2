# Vespoid Princess

## Bio

When a vespoid queen nears death, she births a new queen— called a vespoid princess until maturity. Princesses resemble ordinary vespoids but are much larger and share the queen’s rainbow wings. Unlike the hive-bound queen, a princess often ventures out to hunt, commanding escorts.

They tend to be more aggressive, frequently testing themselves against tougher foes—behavior that shortens their lives. Because many die young, an aging queen may produce several princesses at once. If more than one survives when the queen passes, the princesses fight to the death to decide who rules the hive.

## Actions

### Sting

Melee Weapon Attack: +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 1 piercing damage plus 7 (3d4) poison damage, and the target must succeed on a DC 11 Constitution saving throw or be paralyzed for 1 minute. The target can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success.

### Corrosive Spray

The vespoid sprays corrosive acid at a creature within 15 feet of it. That creature must make a DC 12 Dexterity saving throw, taking 3 (1d6) acid damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one. If the target has a nonmagical weapon made of metal, the weapon takes a permanent and cumulative -1 penalty to damage rolls. If its penalty drops to -5, the weapon is destroyed.
