# Bulaqchi

## Bio

The bulaqchi is a small neopteron resembling a flightless mosquito with a yellow-green carapace and a needle-like mouthpart. It scavenges carrion, injecting digestive fluids and drinking the liquefied tissues; after feeding it becomes bloated and rests on walls or ceilings. Swarms gather around carcasses and advance with short stabs and hops, while lone individuals move slowly.

The forelegs serve as sensitive sensory organs that react to vibration and sudden noise, which can startle the insect into brief immobility. It inhabits open plains and dry ravines, clinging to shaded rock and other surfaces to avoid wind.

The bulaqchi jumps at a creature within 5 feet of it. That creature must succeed on a DC 10 Strength saving throw or be knocked prone and grappled (Escape DC 10). Until this grapple ends, the target is restrained, and the bulaqchi can only use its Drain action.
