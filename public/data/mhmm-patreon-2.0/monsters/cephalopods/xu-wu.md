# Xu Wu

## Bio

The xu wu is an eight-limbed cephalopod with six long, flexible arms and two reduced forelimbs that shield the beak. Golden mucus hardens along its arms into blade-like spikes. Its skin is green-black and gnarled with gold and teal veining. The underside glows bright teal and bristles with spines.

A crown-like crest tops a mushroom-shaped head, while the true mouth opens on the glowing underside. It is blind and hunts by touch and vibration. Xu wu extends its arms to strike at range, carves blades to slash or hurl as projectiles, and repositions with sudden bursts of speed.

It can throw a white mist, flatten its body, and slip past a foe before lunging. When it commits, it tries to pin and swallow targets whole. Xu wu lairs in deep cavern networks beneath ancient ruins. It preys chiefly on construct monsters, favoring wounded or spent targets, and drags kills back to its den to digest.

Spherical hatchlings called xu yo appear in brief swarms after a spawning and then disperse into the tunnels.

## Traits

### Ambusher

The xu wu has advantage on initiative rolls.

### Amphibious

The xu wu can breathe air and water.

### Compression

The xu wu can move through any opening at least 1 inch wide without squeezing, except when it has a creature swallowed. It can also squeeze to fit into a space that a Tiny creature or the creature it has swallowed could fit in.

### Engorged

The xu wu's burrow speed is 0 while it has a creature swallowed.

### Standing Leap

The xu wu's long jump is up to 30 feet and its high jump is up to 15 feet, without a running start.


## Actions

### Multiattack

The xu wu makes four Tentacle attacks or two Spiked Fan attacks.

### Bite

Melee Weapon Attack: +10 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target the xu wu is grappling. Hit: 27 (4d10 + 5) piercing damage. If the target is a Medium or smaller creature, that creature is swallowed, and the grapple ends. While swallowed, the creature is blinded and restrained, it has total cover against attacks and other effects outside the xu wu, and it takes 17 (5d6) acid damage at the start of each of the xu wu's turns. If the xu wu takes 40 damage or more on a single turn from a creature inside it, the xu wu must succeed on a DC 20 Constitution saving throw at the end of that turn or regurgitate all swallowed creatures, which fall prone in a space within 10 feet of the xu wu. If the xu wu dies, a swallowed creature is no longer restrained by it and can escape from the corpse using 10 feet of movement, exiting prone.
