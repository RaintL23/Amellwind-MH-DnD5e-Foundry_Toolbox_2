# Nu Udra

## Bio

The nu udra is a huge cephalopod that moves and fights with six main limbs. Dark green skin carries blue-black striping but usually looks pitch-black under a coat of oily silt. It is mostly blind; bioluminescent stalks and glowing pads at the tips of its six limbs serve as primary sense organs.

It hunts by feel. The arms sweep, probe, and grapple, striking from any angle, even directly behind. It can burrow to ambush or squeeze through cracks to escape. When roused, it ignites its oil coat and fights wreathed in flame. Nu udra favors superheated basins and vent fields where the ground seeps oil and heat.

It lies dormant in deep, hot pockets during cooler periods and roams widely during the burning season. It is solitary and usually avoids conflict unless provoked or the heat drives it to feed. After the burning season, swarms of young briefly appear before dispersing.

## Traits

### Compression

The nu udra can move through any opening at least 1 inch wide without squeezing. It can also squeeze to fit into a space that a Tiny creature could fit in.

### Fire Aura

At the start of each of the nu udra's turns, each creature within 5 feet of it takes 7 (2d6) fire damage, and flammable objects in the aura that aren't being worn or carried ignite. A creature that touches the nu udra or hits it with a melee attack while within 5 feet of it takes 7 (2d6) fire damage.

### Legendary Resistance (2/Day)

If the nu udra fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.

The nu udra flails about, flinging magma in a 30-foot radius around it. Each creature in the area must make a DC 18 Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save, creature's within 15 feet of nu udra take 33 (6d10) bludgeoning damage and be knocked prone and creatures in between 15 and 30 feet take 40 (9d8) fire damage and catch fire. On a successful save, the creature takes half as much damage and isn't knocked prone or doesn't catch fire.


## Legendary Actions

The nu udra can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action option can be used at a time and only at the end of another creature's turn. The nu udra regains spent legendary actions at the start of its turn.

### Swipe

The nu udra twists its main tentacles and swipes around it. Each creature on the ground within 10 feet of the nu udra must succeed on a DC 19 Strength saving throw or be knocked prone.

### Trample (Costs 2 Actions)

The nu udra moves up to its speed. During this move it can move through the spaces of other creatures without provoking opportunity attacks. Each creature the nu udra moves through must succeed on a DC 19 Dexterity saving throw or take 17 (2d10 + 6) bludgeoning damage and be knocked prone.

### Fire Breath (Costs 2 Actions)

The nu udra exhales fire in a 45-foot cone. Each creature in that area must make a DC 18 Dexterity saving throw, taking 17 (5d6) fire damage and catches fire on a failed save, or half as much damage and doesn't catch fire on a successful one.
