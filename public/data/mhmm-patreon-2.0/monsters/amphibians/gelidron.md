# Gelidron

## Bio

The gelidron is a small amphibian that looks like a giant salamander with a rounded head, tiny eyes, and a thick tail. Its skin is dark on the head and blends into blues and pinks, coated in slippery mucus that gets darker in oil and tar. It has sharp claws for climbing, and a sticky pink tongue to quickly catch prey.

Gelidron are mostly nocturnal and eat oil-dwelling crustaceans like crudeshell crabs. When threatened, they try to intimidate with a wide-mouth display, but usually just dive into deeper oil pools to hide. They live in large packs, are hunted by many predators and people for their meat and survive mainly due to fast reproduction.
