# Tigerstripe Zamtrios

## Bio

The tigerstripe zamtrios is an orange-brown variant of the zamtrios with hints of red along its body and blueish coloring on its fins and belly. Its water-retentive skin helps it survive the dry desert dunes, while its coloration provides natural camouflage. Unlike its arctic relative, the tigerstripe zamtrios cannot create ice armor, but it can instantly inflate itself using stored water to roll or leap at enemies, and , and it can spray the water to return to normal size.

It is also able to spit globs of paralyzing fluid. This amphibian makes its home near oases in the dunes. It is highly aggressive and more territorial than its relatives, patrolling their territories when the heat is less intense. These amphibians are top predators, hunting apceros, rhenoplos, kelbi, and hermitaur, and will steal kills from smaller hunters like cephalos and great jaggi.

They also face fierce competition from powerful rivals such as pink rathian, deviljho, rajang, shrouded nerscylla, seregios, and nibelnsarf. Despite being smaller than its icy kin, and its lack of strong natural defenses is offset by its adaptability, mobility, and offensive abilities, allowing it to thrive in the harsh and competitive environment of the dunes.

## Traits

### Amphibious

The zamtrios can breathe air and water.

### Desert Camouflage

The zamtrios has advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks made to hide in desert terrain while burrowed.

### Standing Leap

The zamtrios's long jump is up to 20 feet and its high jump is up to 15 feet, without a running start. In addition, the zamtrios does not incur opportunity attacks while moving with a jump.

### Deadly Leap (Expanded Only)

If the zamtrios jumps at least 15 feet as part of its movement, it can then use this action to land on its feet in a space that contains one or more other creatures. Each of those creatures must succeed on a DC 16 Strength or Dexterity saving throw (target's choice) or be knocked prone and take 25 (6d6 + 4) bludgeoning damage. On a successful save, the creature takes only half the damage, isn't knocked prone, and is pushed 5 feet out of the zamtrios's space into an unoccupied space of the creature's choice. If no unoccupied space is within range, the creature instead falls prone in the zamtrios's space.

### Roll Over (Expanded Only)

The zamtrios can move up to half its movement speed in a straight line, without provoking an attack of opportunity, while rolling over and squishing anything in its path. Each creature in that area must make a DC 16 Dexterity saving throw, taking 21 (5d6 + 4) bludgeoning damage and are knocked prone on a failed save, or half as much damage isn't knocked prone on a successful one.

## Bonus Actions

### Expand (Recharge 4-6)

The zamtrios greatly expands its belly for 1 minute or until it uses Water Beam. While expanded, the zamtrios is Gargantuan, has resistance to bludgeoning damage, and its ice armor is destroyed. If the zamtrios lacks the room to become Gargantuan, it attains the maximum size possible in the space available. The zamtrios can repeat this bonus action to revert back to its original size.


## Legendary Actions

The zamtrios can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action option can be used at a time and only at the end of another creature's turn. The zamtrios regains spent legendary actions at the start of its turn.
