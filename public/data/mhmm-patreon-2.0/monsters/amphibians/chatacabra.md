# Chatacabra

## Bio

The chatacabra is an amphibian with strong, bulky forelimbs and a posture similar to ape-like fanged beasts. Its turquoise scaled body and spiked jaw hides a long tongue, which it uses both for attack and to coat its forelimbs in adhesive saliva. This allows it to attach stones and earth to its arms for extra armor and stronger blows.

As a smaller predator, chatacabra prefer the safety of valleys and canyons where larger predators can't reach, avoiding open areas whenever possible. They primarily feed on swarms of bulaqchi, using their adhesive tongue to catch dozens of these neopterons at once. Despite not being very aggressive, they will attack if you remain in their general vicinity for too long.

## Traits

### Amphibious

The chatacabra can breathe air and water.

### Standing Leap

The chatacabra's long jump is up to 20 feet and its high jump is up to 15 feet, without a running start. In addition, the chatacabra does not provoke opportunity attacks while moving with a jump.


## Actions

### Multiattack

The chatacabra makes two Fist attacks. It can replace one of these attacks with a Lick attack.

### Fist

Melee Weapon Attack: +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 9 (2d6 + 2) bludgeoning damage.

### Lick

Melee Weapon Attack: +4 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 11 (2d8 + 2) acid damage.

### Deadly Leap

If the chatacabra jumps at least 15 feet as part of its movement, it can then use this action to land on its feet in a space that contains one or more other creatures. Each of those creatures must succeed on a DC 12 Strength or Dexterity saving throw (target's choice) or be knocked prone and take 12 (3d6 + 2) bludgeoning damage On a successful save, the creature takes only half the damage, isn't knocked prone, and is pushed 5 feet out of the chatacabra's space into an unoccupied space of the creature's choice. If no unoccupied space is within range, the creature instead falls prone in the chatacabra's space.
