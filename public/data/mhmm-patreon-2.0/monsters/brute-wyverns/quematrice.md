# Quematrice

## Bio

The quematrice is a brute wyvern with green scales and red-blue striping. It has short arms, heavy hind legs, orange eye crests like a mask, a tall head comb, and a throat wattle, giving it a cockatrice look. Its defining trait is flammable tail oil that ignites on impact. this oil, when struck, ignites both the environment and anything unlucky enough to be too close.

It sweeps its tail to send fire across the ground to create large waves of fire to burn its enemies. The quematrice is aggressive and often fights smaller monsters over carrion.

## Actions

### Multiattack

The quematrice makes three Bite attacks or two Tail attacks.

### Bite

Melee Weapon Attack: +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 10 (2d6 + 3) piercing damage.

### Tail

Melee Weapon Attack: +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 16 (3d8 + 3) bludgeoning damage.

### Ignition Swipe (Recharge 5-6)

The quematrice sweeps its tail across a 10-foot long line that is 20 feet wide. Each creature in that line must make a DC 14 Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save, the creature takes 21 (4d8 + 3) bludgeoning damage and catches fire; Until a creature takes an action to douse the fire, the target takes 5 (1d10) fire damage at the start of each of its turns. On a successful save, the creature takes half as much damage and doesn't catch fire.


## Legendary Actions

The quematrice can take 1 legendary action, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action option can be used at a time and only at the end of another creature's turn. The quematrice regains spent legendary actions at the start of its turn.

### Move

The quematrice moves up to half its speed.

### Tail Slam

The quematrice makes one Tail attack that deals 13 (3d6 + 3) bludgeoning damage. If the attack hits and target is a creature, it must succeed on a DC 14 Strength saving throw or be knocked prone. 69
