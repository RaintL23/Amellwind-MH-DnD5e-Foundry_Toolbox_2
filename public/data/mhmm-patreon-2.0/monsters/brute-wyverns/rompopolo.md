# Rompopolo

## Bio

The rompopolo is a brute wyvern with the standard build: small arms, heavy hind legs, and a powerful tail. It bears a long, needle-like beak with a forked tongue, mantis-like foreclaws, and gecko-like toe pads for traction on slick ground. Its eyes are close-set and reddish-violet.

The hide is dark blue-purple, and expandable gas bladders run along the head, back, and tail, vented by insect-like spiracles. The tail ends in a stinger. Its signature adaptation is toxic gas. The bladders fill with an aerosol and vent in controlled bursts that blast soft ground.

Buried prey rushes up, where the rompopolo pins with its claws or spears with its bill. It also probes substrate by touch, using the bill to sense vibration and the tongue to inject toxin into holes. Rompopolo favor wetlands with soft sediments—swamps, bogs, mudflats, and oil-rich basins.

## Traits

### Special

Saving Throw Con +9, Wis +3, Cha +0 Skills Perception +3 Damage Immunities poison Condition Immunities poisoned Senses tremorsense 30 ft., passive Perception 13 Languages — Challenge 8 (3,900 XP) Proficiency +3 Inflated Body. Parts of the rompopolo's body are filled with gas. When the rompopolo is critically hit it must succeed on a DC 15 Strength saving throw or be knocked prone and deflate, releasing poisonous gas in a 5-foot radius around it. Each creature in that area must succeed on DC 17 Constitution saving throw or be poisoned for 1 minute. A poisoned creature can repeat its saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on a success. While deflated, the rompopolo can't use Oil Explosion or Poison Breath and it remains deflated until the end of its next turn.


## Actions

### Multiattack

The rompopolo makes two Claw attacks.

### Claw

Melee Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 15 (3d6 + 5) slashing damage.

### Oil Explosion

The rompopolo injects oil into the ground with its tail causing an explosion of gas at a point within 120 feet of it. Each creature in a 5-foot radius of that point must make a DC 17 Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save, a creature takes 21 (6d6) poison damage and be poisoned for 1 minute. On a successful save, the creature takes half as much damage and isn't poisoned. A poisoned creature can repeat its saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on a success. If the rompopolo is below half its maximum hit points, it can instead use this action to create a large explosion in a 20-foot radius around it.

### Poison Breath (Recharge 5-6)

The rompopolo exhales gas in a 45-foot cone. Each creature in that area must make a DC 17 Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, the creature takes 38 (7d10) poison damage and is poisoned for 1 minute on a failed save or half as much damage and isn't poisoned on a successful one. On a successful save, the creature takes half as much damage and isn't poisoned. A poisoned creature can repeat its saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on a success. 72
