# Zoh Shia

## Bio

Zoh Shia is a guardian-class construct tied to ancient ruins. Its frame shows the hallmarks of guardian constructs: pale plating traced with purple, luminous veins and featureless black eyes. wylk-reactive tissues and fluids give it a cold, crystalline sheen when it stirs. It seldom acts as a prowling predator.

As a wylk-powered construct, it neither feeds nor sleeps. It moves with purpose through deep chambers and wylk pools, rousing dormant guardians and redirecting them like a warden. When the flow wanes, it withdraws to reservoirs until its reserves rebuild. Zoh Shia is most often recorded in the lowest reaches of ruins it is inhabiting, near heavy wylk flow and ancient conduits.

During periods of heightened ruin activity, it ascends to central vaults and causeways, then withdraws when the disturbance passes. Tracks are scarce; its passage is marked more by awakened guardians than by prints. Zoh Shia fights like a living siege engine. It batters foes with claws, tail, and winged-arms, then smashes through lines with trampling charges.

When the wylk surge rises, heat blooms off its frame. It exhales fire, launches burning spheres across the field, and splits the ground with shock lines of wylk that erupt as fire or force. During these surges it moves faster and hits harder. When weakened, it reveals its core state and grows more reckless. wylk spills into the ground and throws up crystal growths that reshape the field before a towering blast of flame detonate them in a cascading explosion.

The crystals can be shattered and will briefly blunt fire for those near them. Between outbursts, wylk sheathing knits damage and hardens over plates. As long as the local flow holds, it restores a buffer of protection and shrugs off lesser blows. When the flow ebbs, it disengages and lets its awakened sentinels close ranks.

## Traits

### True Form (Mythic Trait; Recharges after a Short or Long Rest)

If Zoh Shia would be reduced to 0 hit points, its current hit point total instead resets to 425 (23d20 + 184) hit points, its AC is reduced to 22, it recharges its Large Fireball and Unstable Explosion, regains any expended uses of Legendary Resistance, and is always in its Exposed State. Additionally, Zoh Shia's can now use its the options in the "Mythic Actions" section during the next hour.

### Legendary Resistance (3/Day)

If Zoh Shia fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.

### Wylk Armor

If Zoh Shia didn't enter the Exposed State or its True Form hasn't activated in the last hour, it regains 40 temporary hit points at the start of each of its turns.

### Fireball Barrage

If Zoh Shia's Exposed state activated in the last hour it can fire three fireballs, each at a different point it can see within 120 feet of it. Each creature in a 15-foot radius sphere centered on a point must make a DC 24 Dexterity saving throw, taking 28 (8d6) fire damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one. A creature in the area of two or more fireballs takes the damage only once but has disadvantage on its saving throw.

### Fire Breath

Zoh Shia exhales fire in a 45-foot cone. Each creature in that area must make a DC 24 Dexterity saving throw, taking 36 (8d8) fire damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.

### Large Fireball (Recharge 6)

Zoh Shia exhales a giant sized fireball which explodes at a point it can see within 120 feet of it. Each creature in a 30-foot-radius sphere centered on that point must make a DC 24 Dexterity saving throw, taking 70 (20d6) fire damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.

### Unstable Eruption (Recharges after a Short or Long Rest)

While in its Exposed State and below half its maximum hit points, the Zoh Shia releases a burst of extreme heat pushing each creature within 10 feet of it, 40 feet away from it as it releases a tremendous amount of wylk into the ground, forming large crystals that fit in a 5-foot cubed area every 30 feet in a 300 foot radius around it. Each crystal can be attacked and destroyed (AC 15; 50 HP; Immunity to psychic and poison damage). When a crystal is destroyed, all creatures within 10-feet of it gain resistance to fire damage while they remains in the area. If the creature already has resistance to fire damage, it reduces the damage taken by an extra 20 fire damage. Until the start of its next turn Zoh Shia can't use legendary actions, be pushed or knocked prone, and it gains 100 temporary hit points. On its subsequent turn it must use its action to release a gout of flames in a 150-foot cylinder that is 120 feet high centered on itself. Each creature in that area must succeed on a DC 24 Dexterity saving throw, taking 140 (40d6) fire damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one. If Zoh Shia's True Form activated in the last hour, it can chose to deal half the damage dealt as force damage.


## Legendary Actions

Zoh Shia can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action option can be used at a time and only at the end of another creature's turn. Zoh Shia regains spent legendary actions at the start of its turn.

### Attack

Zoh Shia makes one Claw or Tail attack.

### Wylk Fissure (Costs 2 Actions)

Zoh Shia slams one or both of its winged-arms onto the ground creating an explosion in a 5-foot long line that is 120-feet wide, a 60-foot long line that is 10 feet wide, or in a 20-footcubed area originating from it. Each creature in that area must make a DC 24 Dexterity saving throw, taking 38 (11d6) force damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one. While in its Exposed State or if True Form has 237 activated in the last hour, it can choose to deal fire damage instead of force damage. If its True Form has activated in the last hour, it slams its winged-arms down a second time in a new area as part of the same legendary action.

### Trample (Costs 2 Actions)

Zoh Shia moves up to its speed. During this move it can move through the spaces of other creatures without provoking opportunity attacks. Each creature Zoh Shia moves through must succeed on a DC 24 Dexterity saving throw or take 30 (4d10 + 8) bludgeoning damage and be knocked prone.


## Mythic Actions

If Zoh Shia's True Form trait has activated in the last hour, it can use the options below as legendary actions.

### Lightning Arc (Costs 2 Actions)

Zoh Shia chooses two points that are no further than 60 feet apart that it can see within 120 feet of it. Each creature in that area must succeed on a DC 24 Dexterity saving throw, taking 49 (9d10) lightning damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.

### Rampage

Zoh Shia uses Trample but can only move in a straight line.
