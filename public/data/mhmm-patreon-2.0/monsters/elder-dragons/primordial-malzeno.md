# Primordial Malzeno

## Stat Block

*Huge Dragon (elder), lawful neutral*
- **Armor Class:** 22 (natural armor)
- **Hit Points:** 418 (31d12 + 217)
- **Speed:** 40 ft., fly 80 ft.
- **Saving Throws:** Dex +15, Con +15, Wis +10, Cha +12
- **Skills:** Perception +10, Stealth +15
- **Damage Resistances:** cold; bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing from nonmagical attacks
- **Damage Immunities:** lightning, poison
- **Condition Immunities:** charmed, frightened, paralyzed, poisoned, stunned, unconscious
- **Senses:** darkvision 120 ft., passive Perception 20
- **Languages:** Draconic
- **Challenge:** 25

## Traits

### Qurio Infested (Mythic Trait; Recharges after a Short or Long Rest)

If the malzeno would be reduced to 0 hit points, its current hit point total instead resets to 310 (23d12 + 161) hit points, it regains all expended legendary resistances and it enters an infested state for 1 hour. While in this state, the malzeno can use its Blink, Qurio Swarm attack, Primordial Breath, Primordial Supernova actions, and Omnidirectional Qurio Swarm legendary action.

### Legendary Resistance (3/Day)

If the malzeno fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.


## Actions

### Multiattack

The malzeno makes two Lance Wingarm Attacks or it uses Wingarm Shield and then makes one Tail (Sweep) attack. If the malzeno's Infested trait activated in the last hour, it can make three Qurio Swarm attacks instead.

### Lance Wingarm

Melee Weapon Attack: +15 to hit, reach 20 ft., one target. Hit: 28 (6d6 + 7) piercing damage.

### Tail

Melee Weapon Attack: +15 to hit, reach 15 ft., one target. Hit: 18 (2d10 + 7) bludgeoning damage, and the target must succeed on a DC 23 Constitution saving throw or be afflicted with bloodblight for 1 minute. A creature can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success. Once per turn, the malzeno can cause one of the following additional effects (choose one or roll a d4): 1-2:

### Pierce

The malzeno makes a Tail attack against each creature in a 10-foot line that is 5 feet wide originating from the target. 3-4:

### Sweep

Each creature in a 15-foot-square centered on a space adjacent to the target must succeed on a DC 23 Strength saving throw or be knocked prone.

### Qurio Swarm (Infested Only)

Ranged Weapon Attack: +15 to hit, range 80/320 ft., one target. Hit: 14 (4d6) necrotic damage, and the target must succeed on a DC 23 Constitution saving throw or be afflicted with bloodblight for 1 minute. A creature can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success.

### Wingarm Shield

The malzeno moves up to 20 feet using its wingarm as a shield. During this move it can move through the spaces of other creatures without provoking opportunity attacks. Each creature the malzeno moves through must succeed on a DC 23 Dexterity saving throw or take 28 (6d6 + 7) bludgeoning damage and be knocked prone.

### Primordial Breath (Recharge 5-6; Infested Only)

The malzeno fires its draconic energy into the ground causing multiple explosions in a 60-foot cone or fires lasers in a 90-foot line that is 15 feet wide. Each creature in that area or line must make a DC 23 Dexterity saving throw, taking 42 (12d6) necrotic damage and afflicted with dragonblight for 1 minute on a failed save, or half as much damage and isn't afflicted with dragonblight on a successful one.

### Primordial Supernova (1/Day; Infested Only)

While the malzeno is between 20 and 60 feet off the ground and not in the Berserk State, it can use this action to unleashes countless qurio swarms. Make one attack Qurio Swarm attack against each creature within 120 feet of the malzeno. Until the start of the malzeno's next round, it can only use its legendary actions (Costs 1 Action) to repeat its Qurio Swarm attack against each creature still within 120 feet of it. On its subsequent turn before it moves, the malzeno can use its action to teleport 10 feet above one creature within 120 feet of it, or a space 120 feet from its current location if no creatures remain within 120 feet of it. Immediately after it teleports, the malzeno releases an explosion of energy. Each creature within 60 feet of the malzeno must make a DC 23 Dexterity saving throw, taking 49 (11d8) necrotic damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.


## Bonus Actions

### Blink (Infested Only)

The malzeno magically teleports, along with any equipment it is wearing or carrying, up to 120 feet to an unoccupied space it can see.


## Reactions

### Parry

The malzeno adds 8 to its AC against one melee attack that would hit it. To do so, the malzeno must see the attacker.

### Protection

Malzeno imposes disadvantage on an attack roll against a creature within 10 feet of it, that isn't the malzeno.


## Legendary Actions

The malzeno can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action option can be used at a time and only at the end of another creature's turn. The malzeno regains spent legendary actions at the start of her turn.

### Attack

The malzeno makes one Lance Wingarm attack.

### Crosswing Chop (Cost 2 Actions)

While on the ground, the malzeno briefly rises 5 feet into the air before slamming its wings on the ground in front of it as it lands. Each creature in a 15-foot-cube originating from the malzeno must make a DC 23 Dexterity saving throw, taking 42 (12d6) bludgeoning damage and be pushed up to 10 feet away and knocked prone on a failed save, or half as much damage and isn't pushed away or knocked prone on a successful one.

### Corkscrew (Costs 2 Actions)

The malzeno moves up to half its fly speed in a straight line while spinning. During this move it can move through the spaces of other creatures without provoking opportunity attacks. Each creature the malzeno moves through must make a DC 23 Dexterity saving throw, taking 34 (6d8 + 7) bludgeoning damage and be knocked prone on a failed save, or half as much damage and is pushed 5-feet into an unoccupied space out of the way of the malzeno's path (PC choice). If there are no unoccupied spaces available, the creature automatically fails its saving throw.

### Omnidirectional Qurio Swarms (Infested Only; Costs 2 Actions)

The malzeno releases qurio swarms that travel away from it before dispersing. Each creature within 30 feet of the malzeno must succeed on a DC 23 Dexterity saving throw or take 40 (9d8) necrotic damage and be afflicted with bloodblight for 1 minute. A creature can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success. 181
