## Traits

### Greater Wind Barrier

A barrier of strong wind surrounds the amatsu in a 10-foot radius around it. The strong wind keeps fog, smoke, and other gases at bay. Small or smaller flying creatures or objects can't pass through the barrier. When a Medium flying creature starts its turn within the barrier or when it enters it for the first time on a turn, it must succeed on a DC 22 Strength saving throw be pushed out of the barrier and can't attempt to pass through it until the start of its next turn. Loose, lightweight materials brought into the barrier fly upward. Arrows, bolts, and other ordinary projectiles launched at the amatsu are deflected upward and automatically miss. (Magic arrows, boulders hurled by Giants or siege engines, and similar projectiles, are unaffected.) Creatures in Gaseous Form can't pass through the barrier.

### Living Storm

The amatsu is always at the center of a storm 1d6 + 4 miles in diameter. Heavy precipitation in the form of either rain or snow falls there, causing the area to be lightly obscured. Heavy rain also extinguishes open flames and imposes disadvantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on hearing. In addition, strong winds swirl in the area covered by the storm. The winds impose disadvantage on ranged attack rolls. The winds extinguish open flames and disperse fog.

### Legendary Resistance (3/Day)

If the amatsu fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.


## Actions

### Multiattack

The amatsu makes one Tail attack and two Water Beam attacks.

### Bite

Melee Weapon Attack: +14 to hit, reach 15 ft., one target. Hit: 19 (3d8 + 6) piercing damage.

### Tail

Melee Weapon Attack: +14 to hit, reach 20 ft., one target. Hit: 28 (4d10 + 6) bludgeoning damage, and the target must succeed on a DC 22 Strength saving throw or be knocked prone.

### Water Beam

Ranged Weapon Attack: +14 to hit, range 150/600 ft., one target. Hit: 23 (5d8) acid damage.

### Water Bubble

Amatsu expels a giant water bubble at a point within 120 feet of it. Each creature in a 20-foot radius sphere centered on that point must make a DC 22 Dexterity saving throw, taking 42 (12d6) acid damage and be afflicted with waterblight for 1 minute on a failed save, or half as much damage and isn't afflicted on a successful one.

### Wind Vortex (Recharge 5-6)

The amatsu conjures a swirling vortex of hurricane winds centered on itself. Each creature within 100 feet of the amatsu must make a DC 22 Strength saving throw or be pulled 30 feet towards the amatsu. A creature that is pulled within 30 feet of the amatsu or is already within that range must make a DC 22 Strength saving throw or be thrown 160 feet straight up into the air on a failed save, or half as high on successful one. If a thrown target strikes a solid surface upon landing, the target takes 3 (1d6) bludgeoning damage for every 10 feet it fell.

### Grand Vortex (Recharge 6)

The amatsu exhales three high pressure beams of water in different directions in front of it. Each beam is a 90-foot line that is 10 feet wide. Each creature in a line must make a DC 22 Dexterity saving throw, taking 82 (15d10) acid damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one. If the amatsu is in its supercharged state, it fires a second beam adjacent to the original.

### Calamity Storm (Storm God State Only; Recharges after a Long Rest)

Lightning continuously crackles and strikes the ground centered on a point directly below the amatsu. Each creature that starts or ends its turn in a 300-foot-radius area centered on that point takes 31 (9d6) lightning damage. Until the start of its next turn, the amatsu can't use its legendary actions and is immune to the incapacitated, stunned, paralyzed, and unconscious conditions. On its next turn, the amatsu can use its action to release an explosion of lightning around it. Each creature within 120 feet of the amatsu must make a DC 22 Dexterity saving throw, taking 99 (22d8) lightning damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.


## Legendary Actions

The amatsu can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action option can be used at a time and only at the end of another creature's turn. The amatsu regains spent legendary actions at the start of its turn.

### Bite Attack

The amatsu makes a Bite attack.

### Wind Wall

The amatsu forms a barrier of wind around itself until the end of its next turn, or after it uses an action or another legendary action. While the barrier is present the amatsu is heavily obscured, it’s speed is reduced to 0, it has advantage on its next attack roll, and creatures have disadvantage on saving throws against effects created by the amatsu (except calamity storm).

### Tail Spin (Costs 2 Actions)

The amatsu makes one Tail attack against each creature within 10 feet of it. A creature that fails its saving throw is also pushed up to 10 feet away.

### Whirlwind (Costs 3 Actions)

The amatsu chooses three unoccupied 5-foot cubes within 5 feet of it. An elemental force that resembles a dust devil appears in the cubes and move in a straight line 60 feet away from the amatsu before dispersing. Each creature in a line must make a DC 22 Strength saving throw, taking 36 (8d8) thunder damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one.


## Mythic Actions

If the amatsu's Storm God State trait has activated in the last hour, it can use the options below as legendary actions.

### Lightning Shurikens

The amatsu creates whips two wheels of lightning energy at different creatures within 60 feet of it. Each creature must make a DC 22 Dexterity saving throw, taking 31 (9d6) lightning damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.

### Lightning Storm (Costs 2 Actions)

The amatsu calls down lightning.
