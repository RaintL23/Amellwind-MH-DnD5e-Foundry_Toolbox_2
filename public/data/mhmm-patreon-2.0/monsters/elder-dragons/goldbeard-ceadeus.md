# Goldbeard Ceadeus

## Bio

Although its relationship with regular ceadeus is unknown, this subspecies is seemingly a fully-grown ceadeus. The goldbeard ceadeus is of a darker coloring, having developed a lustrous golden hide and darker brown underbelly, with a more pronounced mane. Unlike the regular ceadeus, both of its horns are large and symmetrical, overtaking its remaining eye.

It is unknown what the feeding habits of a goldbeard ceadeus is, like most other Elder Dragons, so it is hard to say where it fits in the ecological niche. Recently, it was discovered that young ceadeus only have one known predator, nakarkos. This predator is believed to also become a food source for the goldbeard.

The goldbeard ceadeus has many of the same adaptions as ceadeus, though both have some differences. A goldbeard ceadeus's color is said to be caused by the luminous bacteria, making it glow a shiny gold. From its shiny color, it lives in deeper waters. The goldbeard ceadeus's eyes are covered by its huge horns now.

It is unknown how it can still see or sense creatures around it. Unlike ceadeus, goldbeard ceadeus never has to come up for air from the many pieces of algae it has grown.
