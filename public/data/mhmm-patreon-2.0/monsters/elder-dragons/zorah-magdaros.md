# Zorah Magdaros

## Bio

The zorah magdaros is a colossal semi-aquatic elder dragon with a rocky hide and turtle-like characteristics, including a mountainous shell on its back and a beaked mouth. Fire, smoke, and lava constantly emanate from its shell, giving it the appearance of a walking volcano. Its magma cores, located on its back and head, are key weak points.

The zorah magdaros’s diet consists of minerals containing explosive materials, which it absorbs and processes into energy, contributing to its fiery nature. Despite its immense size and destructive potential, it is relatively docile and tends to ignore most threats, although it will retaliate with massive physical blows if sufficiently provoked.

This elder dragon is capable of causing significant geological changes simply by moving through an area. Its body constantly spews magma from crater-like vents, which it can weaponize into devastating attacks. Researchers propose that what are perceived as normal volcanoes might be dormant zorah magdaros, explaining the rarity of sightings.

Despite its gentle nature, the zorah magdaros will fiercely defend itself if necessary, using its immense strength to crush any perceived threats. The zorah magdaros’s presence has a profound ecological impact, often reshaping landscapes and creating opportunities for other monsters to inhabit newly formed environments.

Smaller creatures are known to follow it and even live on the shell that covers its back, benefiting from the heat and protection it offers. However, the elder dragon is not entirely without threats, as it is occasionally attacked by the formidable nergigante. When nearing death, the zorah magdaros seeks out a remote area to release all the energy within its body in a great fiery explosion that can drastically alter ecosystems.

This explosion leaves behind a transformed landscape, creating new habitats for other creatures. Overall, the zorah magdaros is a gentle giant with the potential to cause massive environmental changes, leaving a lasting legacy on the ecosystems it traverses. ## Traits

### Casual Stroll

the zorah magdaros cannot take the Dash action, unless it is fleeing.

### Epic Creature

The zorah magdaros makes all ability checks, attack rolls, and saving throws with advantage. If the zorah magdaros rolls a 1 while making an attack, it does not count as a critical failure. In addition, all of the zorah magdaros’s senses extend up to five miles.

### Fire Aura

At the start of each of the zorah magdaros's turns, each creature within 50 feet of it takes 10 (3d6) fire damage, and flammable objects in the aura that aren't being worn or carried ignite. A creature that touches the zorah magdaros or hits it with a melee attack while within 5 feet of it takes 10 (3d6) fire damage. While the zorah's shell is still intact, the aura deals an extra 3d6 fire damage and the 200 feet above it is part of the aura's area. The aura's area above the zorah magdaros and extra damage is reduced by 50 feet and 1d6 fire damage each time the shell's hit points are reduced by 25% Magic Resistance. The zorah magdaros has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

### Natural Instinct

zorah magdaros ignores Medium or smaller creatures and objects that are not on its body, in its path, or that have not harmed it in the last 10 minutes. When all three magma cores are destroyed, the zorah magdaros will attempt to flee.

### Siege Monster

The zorah magdaros deals double damage to objects and structures.

### Siege Vulnerability

Siege weapon damage bypasses the zorah magdaros's immunities and resistances (including body parts). Actions and Legendary Actions On its turn, the zorah magdaros can take 6 actions, choosing from the options below. In addition, the zorah magdaros can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the same options. Only one legendary action can be used at a time and only at the end of another creature’s turn. The zorah magdaros regains spent legendary actions at the start of its turn.

### Claws

The zorah magdaros can use its Head to attack with one of its Claws.

### Frightful Presence (1/Turn)

The zorah magdaros can use its Head to use its Frightful Presence.

### Head (Costs 2 Actions, 2/Turn)

The zorah magdaros can use its Head to use its Lava Breath (if recharged) or Bite.

### Magma Core (1/Turn per Core)

The zorah magdaros can use its Magma Core to use Lava Eruption.

### Move

The zorah magdaros can use its Head to move with its Legs.

### Shell (Costs 2 Actions)

The zorah magdaros can use its head to attack with its Lava Projectiles or use its Erupt (if Recharged).

The zorah magdaros targets a point that it can see within 55 feet of it, biting everything in a 15-foot cube centered on that point. If the zorah magdaros dies, a swallowed creature is no longer restrained by it and can escape from the corpse by using 30 feet of movement, exiting prone.

### Frightful Presence

Each creature of the zorah magdaros's choice that is within 1000 feet of the zorah magdaros and aware of it must succeed on a DC 17 Wisdom saving throw or become frightened for 1 minute. A creature can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success. If a creature's saving throw is successful or the effect ends for it, the creature is immune to the zorah magdaros's Frightful Presence for the next 24 hours.
