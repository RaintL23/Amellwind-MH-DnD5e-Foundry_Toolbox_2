# Savage Omega Planetes

## Stat Block

*Huge construct, chaotic neutral*
- **Armor Class:** 20 (natural armor)
- **Hit Points:** 351 (26d12 + 182)
- **Speed:** 50 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 25 | 16 | 24 | 22 | 18 | 10 |

- **Damage Resistances:** cold
- **Damage Immunities:** fire, poison, psychic; bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing from nonmagical weapons that aren't adamantine
- **Condition Immunities:** blinded, charmed, deafened, exhaustion, frightened, paralyzed, petrified, poisoned, prone, unconscious
- **Senses:** truesight 120 ft., passive Perception 14
- **Languages:** all
- **Proficiency Bonus:** +7
- **Challenge:** 23 (50,000 XP or 100,000 XP as a mythic encounter)

## Traits

### Pantokrator Mode (Mythic Trait; Recharges after a Short or Long Rest)

If the omega planetes would be reduced to 0 hit points, its current hit point total instead resets to 351 (26d12 + 182) hit points, it recharges its Recharge Attack, regains all expended legendary resistances, creates a clone nerscylla in a space within 30 feet of it, and enters a Charging State for four rounds.

While charging the omega planetes is immune to all damage and other effects, its movement is reduced to 0, and it can't use actions, reactions, or legendary actions. At the start of its turn on the fourth round it uses its Giant Laser, but the area is a 300-foot radius-sphere around it. When the clone nerscylla is defeated, it creates a 20-foot radius sphere force field as if by the omega planetes' Shield Generator. The force field lasts until the omega planetes uses Giant Laser, or for one round if the laser has already been fired.

### Immutable Form

The omega planetes is immune to any spell or effect that would alter its form.

### Legendary Resistance (3/Day)

If the omega planetes fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.

### Magic Resistance

The omega planetes has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

### Magic Weapons

The omega planetes's weapon attacks are magical.

### Mark of Emnity

The omega planetes targets creature wielding shields with its melee weapon attacks except when a creature critically hits the omega planetes, it will instead target that creature until the end of its next turn.

### Shield Generator

When the omega planetes takes 30 or more damage from a single attack or is critically hit, it will drop a shield generator on the ground in an unoccupied space within 5 feet of it.

A creature that picks up the shield generator can use an action to activate it, creating a force field in a 10-foot-cubed area around it. The force field lasts for 1 minute and when a creature must make a saving throw against an effect from the omega planetes while in it, it automatically succeeds on the save and takes no damage from it; the force field then shatters.


## Actions

### Multiattack

The omega planetes makes three attacks, each of which can be a Slam or a Laser Blast attack. It can replace any of these attacks with a use of Ballistic Impact.

### Slam

Melee Weapon Attack: +14 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 21 (4d6 + 7) bludgeoning damage. If the target is a creature, it must succeed on a DC 22 Strength saving throw or be knocked prone.

### Laser Blast

Ranged Weapon Attack: +14 to hit, range 240 ft., one target. Hit: 17 (3d6 + 7) force damage.

### Atomic Ray

The omega planetes shoots a fan of lasers in a 30-foot cone from one side of its body. It fires an additional set of lasers from another side of its body each time its hit points are reduced by 25% of its maximum hit points. Each creature in a cone must make a DC 22 Dexterity saving throw, taking 28 (8d6) force damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.

### Ballistic Impact

The omega planetes launches a bomb into the air at a point within 120 feet of it. Each creature in a 10-foot-radius sphere centered on that point must succeed on a DC 22 Dexterity saving throw or take 16 (3d10) fire damage and catch fire. Until a creature takes an action to douse the fire, the target takes 9 (2d8) fire damage at the start of each of its turns.

### Deadly Leap

If the omega planetes jumps at least 10 feet as part of its movement, it can then use this action to land on its feet in a space that contains one or more other creatures. Each of those creatures must succeed on a DC 22 Strength or Dexterity saving throw (target's choice) or be knocked prone and take 22 (4d10 + 7) bludgeoning damage. On a successful save, the creature takes only half the damage, isn't knocked prone, and is pushed 5 feet out of the omega planetes's space into an unoccupied space of the creature's choice. If no unoccupied space is within range, the creature instead falls prone in the omega planetes's space.

### Recharge Attack (Recharge 5-6)

The omega planetes uses one of the following recharge attacks:

### Giant Laser

The omega planetes fires a giant laser in a 150-foot line that is 15 feet wide. Each creature in that area must make a DC 22 Dexterity saving throw, taking 99 (18d10) force damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.

### Wave Cannon

The omega planetes fires numerous lasers in one half of a 30-foot-radius circle. Each creature in that area must make a DC 22 Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save, the target takes 81 (18d8) force damage and is knocked prone. On a successful save, the target takes half as much damage and isn't knocked prone.

### Mustard Bomb

The omega planetes targets one creature within 120 feet of it. Until the end of the creature's next turn, the area around it flashes red. At the end of its turn, fire covers the ground in a 20-foot radius around it for 1 minute. The creature can make a DC 22 Dexterity saving throw, moving 10 feet into an unoccupied space on a failed save or 25 feet on a successful one.

A creature that starts its turn or enters the area for the first time on a turn takes 35 (10d6) fire damage and catches fire. Until a creature takes an action to douse the fire, the target takes 9 (2d8) fire damage at the start of each of its turns.


## Legendary Actions

The omega planetes can take 4 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action option can be used at a time and only at the end of another creature's turn. The omega planetes regains spent legendary actions at the start of its turn.

### Flamethrower

The omega planetes releases a gout of flame in a 5-foot radius around it. Each creature in that area must make a DC 22 Dexterity saving throw, taking 11 (2d10) fire damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.

### Trample (Costs 2 Actions)

The omega planetes moves up to its speed. During this move it can move through the spaces of other creatures without provoking opportunity attacks. Each creature the omega planetes moves through must succeed on a DC 22 Dexterity saving throw or take 18 (2d10 + 7) bludgeoning damage and be knocked prone.

### HP Penalty (Costs 2 Actions)

The omega planetes shoots an orb at the target of its Mark of Emnity. That creature must make a DC 22 Constitution saving throw, taking 14 (4d6) necrotic damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one. The target's hit point maximum is reduced by an amount equal to the necrotic damage taken. The reduction lasts until the target finishes a long rest. The target dies if this effect reduces its hit point maximum to 0.

### MRV Missile (Costs 3 Actions)

The omega planetes fires a bomb at each creature within 120 feet of it. Each creature must succeed on a DC 22 Dexterity saving throw or take 21 (6d6) fire damage and catch fire. Until a creature takes an action to douse the fire, the target takes 9 (2d8) fire damage at the start of each of its turns.

### Omega Microbes

The omega planetes summons 7 (2d6) omega microbes, each appearing in an unoccupied space within 30 feet of it.

### Expel Frost

Each creature within 15 feet of the omega planetes must make a DC 22 Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, the creature takes 35 (10d6) cold damage and is afflicted with frostblight for 1 minute. On a successful save, the creature takes half as much damage and isn't afflicted with frostblight. A creature can repeat its saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on a success.


## Mythic Actions

### Clone Nerscylla

A clone nerscylla uses the tempered nerscylla with the following changes:

- It gains the effects of the blur spell.
- Its poisonous maw attack deals force damage instead of poison damage.
- Replace its Stinger with the following attack.

### Laser Beam (Recharge 5-6)

The nerscylla fires a beam of magic energy in a 60-foot long line that is 5 feet wide or across a 30-foot cone. Each creature in that area must make a DC 17 Dexterity saving throw, taking 28 (8d6) force damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.

### Lair Actions

On initiative count 20 (losing initiative ties), the omega planetes takes a lair action to cause one of the following effects; the omega planetes can't use the same effect two rounds in a row:

### Rocket Punch

The omega planetes launches rocket fists at each creature of its choice within 120 feet of it. The fists can be attacked and destroyed (AC 10; hp 15; immunity to poison and psychic damage). On the subsequent initiative 20, the fists hit its target, dealing 21 (6d6) bludgeoning damage.
