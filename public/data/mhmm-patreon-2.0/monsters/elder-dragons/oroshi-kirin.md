# Oroshi Kirin

## Bio

Oroshi kirin bears a dark navy coat with violet striping. Its mane runs black with pale purple tips, its eyes shine red, and the horn is larger than a typical kirin’s, that is pink-tinged and tightly spiraled from base to tip. These features mark it immediately apart from the thunder-aligned kirin.

Through that horn it manipulates cold, dropping ambient temperature and freezing moisture in the air. In battle it calls up lances of ice, veils of freezing fog, and sudden sheets of rime that punish any approach. It is more aggressive than a typical kirin, favoring short, slashing charges through its own mist before the next burst of frost.

Field reports place sightings in isolated highlands and deep forests, especially during severe cold snaps. It is solitary and elusive, leaving little sign beyond hoarfrost and brittle, needle-sharp ice. Some naturalists argue the form may be a mutation rather than a stable subspecies, but evidence is sparse outside rare encounters.

Hunters’ tales of valleys flash-frozen overnight may be exaggeration, yet they fit the oppressive chill that follows in its wake. When the air stills, its freezing fog lingers, turning ground treacherous and muffling sound until the kirin strikes again. ### Flash Freeze

The kirin magically creates three giant ice glaciers, each of which erupts from under a target the kirin can see within 120 feet of it. A target must make a DC 21 Dexterity saving throw, taking 18 (4d8) cold damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.

### Freezing Fog

The kirin conjures a 15-foot-radius sphere of icy fog centered on one of the spaces it occupies for 1 minute. A creature that starts its turn or enters the fog for the first time on a turn, takes 10 (3d6) cold damage and must succeed on a DC 21 Constitution saving throw or be frozen. A creature that is frozen, has its body encased in ice and snow. This ice and snow can be attacked and destroyed (AC 10; hp 15; vulnerability to fire damage; immunity to cold damage), ending the frozen condition on the creature. The frozen creature can also use an action to make a DC 21 Strength check. On a successful check, the creature breaks free and is no longer frozen.

### Frozen Daggers

The kirin magically creates ice that erupts from the ground in a 90-foot line that is 5 feet wide. Each creature in that line must make a DC 21 Dexterity saving throw, taking 31 (7d8) cold damage and is afflicted with iceblight for 1 minute on a failed save, or half as much damage and is not afflicted with iceblight on a successful one. A creature can make a DC 21 Constitution saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success.

### Spellcasting

The kirin casts one of the following spells, requiring no material components and using Wisdom as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 21, +13 to hit with spell attacks): At will: control weather, expeditious retreat, ice knife 3/day: ice storm 1/day each: investiture of ice*, wall of ice *The kirin casts this spell on itself before combat.
