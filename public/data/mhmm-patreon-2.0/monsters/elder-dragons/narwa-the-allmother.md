# Narwa the Allmother

## Bio

The Allmother gains eight gold orbs in her belly thundersac, with several thundersacs and tails elongating and becoming bright purple. Her eyes alternate between yellow and red, and her golden skin darkens to a deep purple, similar to Apex Monsters. The Allmother is typically found suspended upside-down in the air, resembling her mate.

The Allmother achieves her form by consuming Wind Serpent Ibushi's lifeforce, allowing her to harness the power to control wind and enhance her electricity abilities. This transformation also fertilizes the eggs inside her. Her attacks become stronger, faster, and have a wider range, incorporating both lightning and wind abilities.

Her electromagnetism allows her to lift buried Dragonators and summon floating stone platforms, while her control over wind creates strong gales. Much more aggressive than her Thunder Serpent form, the Allmother is driven by a desire to protect her fertilized eggs from danger. She perceives all other life as threats to her offspring and will do anything to protect them.

In this state, she is a formidable threat on an ecosystem-wide scale, capable of easily defeating apex predators and Elder Dragons. The Allmother primarily resides in the Coral Palace to guard her eggs, her presence drawing other monsters either attracted to her power or aiming to end the Rampage.

Despite their efforts, the Allmother can overwhelm and defeat them with her enhanced abilities.

## Traits

### Legendary Resistance (3/Day)

If the narwa fails a saving throw, she can choose to succeed instead.

### Magic Resistance

The narwa has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

### Magnetism (1/round)

The first time the narwa uses an action, legendary action, or effect that deals lightning damage on a turn, she causes four chunks of terrain within 60 feet of it to rise into the air until the start of its next turn. The raised terrain fills a 10-foot squared by 5-foot deep area. If there are large objects made of metal, like ballistae or pockets of ore, those areas targeted first by this trait.

### Resonance

The first time an elf, wyverian, creature who can deal psychic damage, or has psionic abilities enters a location 1 mile of the narwa, it must succeed on a DC 25 Intelligence or Wisdom saving throw (target's choice). A creature with psionic abilities makes its save with advantage. On a successful save, the target resonates with the narwa. When the resonating creature is within 1 mile of the narwa, its eyes glow a yellow color, it can hear the narwa's thoughts. The creature can also sense the narwa's presence and may speak the narwa's thoughts out loud when it is within 600 feet of it. On a failed save, the creature does not resonate with the narwa and is immune to the narwa's Resonance forever.

### Siege Monster

The narwa deals double damage to objects and structures.


## Actions

### Multiattack

The narwa makes two Bite attacks and then uses Spinning Coil Burst.

### Bite

Melee Weapon Attack: +17 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 26 (4d8 + 8) piercing damage. If the target is a Large or smaller creature, it must succeed on a DC 24 Strength saving throw or be pushed 10 feet away from the narwa.

### Tail

Melee Weapon Attack: +17 to hit, reach 20 ft., one target. Hit: 30 (4d10 + 8) bludgeoning damage. Then the target and each creature in a 5-foot line that is 60 feet wide must make a DC 26 Dexterity saving throw, taking 28 (8d6) lightning damage on a failed save, or half as much on a successful one.

### Spinning Coil Burst

The narwa unleashes a number of rotating lightning coils in a 90-foot cone in front of her. Each creature in that area must make a DC 26 Dexterity saving throw, taking 21 (6d6) lightning damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.

### Thunder Bomb (Recharge 5-6)

The narwa unleashes a massive ball of lightning in a 120-foot line that is 10 feet wide, or until it strikes a solid surface or creature where it explodes in a 30-foot-radius sphere. Each creature in the explosions area must make a DC 26 Dexterity saving throw, taking 28 (8d6) lightning damage plus 28 (8d6) thunder damage on a failed save, or half as much on a successful one.

### Allmother Annihilation (1/day)

The narwa summons multiple dragonators that spin radially outwards from it, both clockwise and counterclockwise until the start of its next turn. Each creature in a 60-foot-radius, 40foot-high cylinder centered on the narwa must make a DC 26 Strength or Dexterity saving throw, taking 55 (10d10) piercing damage plus 22 (5d8) thunder damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one. If the save fails by 5 or more, the creature is also pulled into the closest unoccupied space to the narwa. A creature that attempts to move away from the narwa while in the area must succeed 210 on a DC 26 Strength saving throw or have its speed reduced to 0 until the start of its next turn. On her subsequent turn, the narwa uses her action to release a giant thunder bomb. Each creature within 60 feet of the narwa must make a DC 26 Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save, creatures within 20 feet of the narwa takes 90 (20d8) lightning damage and creatures further than 20 feet take 54 (12d8) lightning damage. On a successful save, the creature takes half as much damage determined by how far away it is from the narwa.


## Legendary Actions

The narwa can take 2 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action option can be used at a time and only at the end of another creature's turn. The narwa regains spent legendary actions at the start of her turn.

### Wind and Thunder Combo (1/round)

The narwa slams its body on the ground, creating five explosions of wind and lightning that begin in a space adjacent to the narwa. The explosions each travel 60 feet in a straight line, in a different direction away from narwa. Each creature in an explosions area must succeed on a DC 26 Dexterity saving throw, taking 24 (7d6) lightning damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.

### Charged Corkscrew (1/round)

The narwa moves up to her fly speed in a straight line while spinning and releasing coils of lightning that expand outward from it. During this move she can move through the spaces of other creatures without provoking opportunity attacks. Each creature the narwa moves through must make a DC 24 Dexterity saving throw, taking 21 (3d8 + 8) bludgeoning damage and be knocked prone on a failed save, or half as much damage and is pushed 5-feet into an unoccupied space out of the way of the narwa's path (PC choice). If there are no unoccupied spaces available, the creature automatically fails its saving throw. Each creature within 20-feet of the narwa's path, but not in it, must succeed on a DC 26 dexterity saving throw or take 18 (4d8) lightning damage.

### Arc Cluster (1/round)

The narwa slams its fist into the ground creating a barrage of lightning in a 45-foot cone. Each creature in that area must make a DC 26 Dexterity saving throw, taking 21 (6d6) lightning damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.

### Lightning Cannon (Costs 2 Actions)

The narwa exhales lightning in a 90-foot line that is 5 feet wide. Each creature in that line must succeed on a DC 26 Dexterity saving throw or take 56 (16d6) lightning damage and be afflicted by thunderblight for 1 minute on a failed save, or half as much damage and isn't afflicted with thunderblight on a successful one.

### Spark Blast (Costs 2 Actions)

The narwa electrifies the air in a 40-foot long, 20-foot wide area that combusts in a cascade of lightning blasts. Each creature in that area must succeed on a DC 26 Constitution saving throw, taking 49 (11d8) lightning damage and be stunned until the end of its next turn on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one and isn't stunned. 211 Allmother Material Bonus* The allmother materials count as narwa materials for her set bonus.
