# Tempered Gogmazios

## Stat Block

*Gargantuan dragon (elder), unaligned*
- **Armor Class:** 24 (22 in Heated State)
- **Hit Points:** 533 (26d20 + 260)
- **Speed:** 60 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 30 | 10 | 30 | 18 | 17 | 23 |

- **Saving Throws:** Dex +8, Wis +11, Cha +14
- **Damage Resistances:** bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage except while in its Heated State
- **Damage Immunities:** acid, cold, lightning
- **Condition Immunities:** blinded, charmed, deafened, exhaustion, frightened, stunned
- **Senses:** darkvision 120 ft., passive Perception 13
- **Languages:** Draconic
- **Proficiency Bonus:** +8
- **Challenge:** 28

## Traits

### Heated State

When the Gogmazios takes 60 or more fire damage on a turn it enters a Heated State until the end of its next turn. While in this state, its Claw attack deals an extra 1d10 fire damage (included in the attack), the tar it places down ignites and explodes on initiative 20, and it takes an extra 10 necrotic damage whenever it takes necrotic damage.

### Hellfire

Fire damage dealt by the gogmazios bypasses fire resistance and deals half damage to creatures that are immune to fire damage.

### Highly Combustible

When a tarred creature is subjected to fire damage, it takes an extra 11 (2d10) fire damage if the tarred condition was caused by the gogmazios. If the gogmazios is in its Heated State, the tar also explodes when it ignites. Each creature in a 5-foot radius of the tar must succeed on a DC 26 Dexterity saving throw or take 11 (2d10) fire damage and be pushed 5 feet away.

### Legendary Resistance (3/Day)

If the gogmazios fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.

### Pitch (Heated State Only)

At the start of each of its turns, the gogmazios secretes tar in 1d10 spaces within 10 feet of it and in two spaces for every 10 feet the gogmazios moves. Each creature that enters the area or starts its turn in that area must make a DC 26 Strength saving throw or be tarred.

### Super-Heated State (Mythic Trait; Recharges after a Short or Long Rest)

When the gogmazios is reduced to 0 hit points, it immediately saves against all ongoing conditions and effects and instantly changes into its Super-Heated Form. Its initiative count doesn't change. While in Super-Heated Form, its speed becomes 60 ft., fly 120 ft., its Armor Class is 22, it loses damage resistances to nonmagical bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing, Pitch applies every turn (not only in Heated State), and it can use the mythic actions below.


## Actions

### Multiattack

The gogmazios makes one Claw attack, one Tail attack, and one Winged Arm attack. It can't make a Claw or Winged Arm attack against the same target as its Tail attack. It can replace two attacks to use one of its Swipe Actions.

### Bite

Melee Weapon Attack: +18 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 23 (3d8 + 10) piercing damage, and the target is tarred (escape DC 18). If the gogmazios is in its Heated State, the tar ignites on initiative 20.

### Claw

Melee Weapon Attack: +18 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 17 (2d6 + 10) slashing damage plus 5 (1d10) fire damage if the gogmazios is in its Heated State.

### Tail

Melee Weapon Attack: +18 to hit, reach 20 ft., one target. Hit: 28 (4d8 + 10) bludgeoning damage, and the target is tarred (escape DC 18).

### Winged Arm

Melee Weapon Attack: +18 to hit, reach 15 ft., one target. Hit: 19 (2d8 + 10) bludgeoning damage. If the target is a creature, it must succeed on a DC 26 Strength saving throw or be knocked prone.

### Swipe Action

The gogmazios uses one of the following Swipe actions:

### Winged Arm Swipe

The gogmazios swipes its winged arm across a 15-foot long line that is 30 feet wide. Each creature in that line must make a DC 26 Dexterity saving throw, taking 18 (4d8) bludgeoning damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one. If the gogmazios is in its Heated State, it ignites the tarred ground in the area.

### Tail Swipe

The gogmazios swipes its tail in a 20-foot cone. The area is covered in tar, and each creature in that area must make a DC 26 Dexterity saving throw, taking 27 (6d8) bludgeoning damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one. If the gogmazios is in its Heated State, the tar ignites on initiative 20.

### Breath Weapons (Recharge 5-6)

The gogmazios uses one of the following breath weapons:

### Fire Breath

The gogmazios exhales fire in a 150-foot line that is 10 feet wide. Each creature in that line must make a DC 26 Dexterity saving throw, taking 55 (10d10) fire damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.

### Oil Breath

The gogmazios spews tar in a 120-foot cone. That area becomes tarred for one hour, or until the tar explodes. Each creature that enters the area for the first time on a turn or starts its turn in that area must make a DC 26 Strength saving throw or be tarred (escape DC 18). If the gogmazios is in its Heated State, it instead spews the tar in a 120-foot line that is 10 feet wide and the tar ignites on initiative 20.


## Legendary Actions

The gogmazios can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action option can be used at a time and only at the end of another creature's turn. The gogmazios regains spent legendary actions at the start of its turn.

### Lunging Attack

The gogmazios moves up to 10 feet. It then makes a Bite attack.

### Ground Slam (Costs 2 Actions)

The gogmazios rises up and slams its winged arms down on the ground beneath it. Each creature in a 20-foot square originating from the gogmazios must make a DC 26 Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save, the creature takes 32 (4d10 + 10) bludgeoning damage, is pushed up to 10 feet away and knocked prone. On a successful save, the creature takes half as much damage, isn't pushed away, or knocked prone.

### Trample (Costs 2 Actions)

The gogmazios moves up to its speed. During this move it can move through the spaces of other creatures without provoking opportunity attacks. Each creature the gogmazios moves through must succeed on a DC 26 Dexterity saving throw or take 19 (2d8 + 10) bludgeoning damage and be knocked prone.


## Mythic Actions

While in Super-Heated Form, the gogmazios can use the options below as legendary actions, and its Multiattack becomes two Claw attacks and one Tail attack (it can't make a Claw attack against the same target as its Tail attack; it can replace two attacks to use one of its Swipe Actions). Bite, Claw, Tail, and Winged Arm attacks always apply heated tar (ignites on initiative 20). Fire Breath can be a 150-foot line that is 10 feet wide, a 60-foot line that is 30 feet wide, or a 15-foot radius around it.

### Attack

The gogmazios makes one Winged Arm attack or moves up to 10 feet and then makes one Bite attack.

### Ground Slam (Costs 2 Actions)

The gogmazios rises up and slams its winged arms down on the ground beneath it. Each creature in a 20-foot square originating from the gogmazios must make a DC 26 Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save, the creature takes 32 (4d10 + 10) bludgeoning damage plus 5 (1d10) fire damage, is pushed up to 10 feet away and knocked prone. On a successful save the creature takes half as much damage, isn't pushed away, or knocked prone.

### Tar Fire Barrage (Costs 2 Actions)

The gogmazios fires balls of molten tar at a point within 60 feet of it, covering the ground in a 30-foot radius in tar. On initiative 20 the tar ignites.

### Winged Arm Sweep (Costs 2 Actions)

The gogmazios flies 10 feet into the air, then drags one of its wings along the ground in a 40-foot line that is 5 feet wide and 10 feet tall. It can land at the end of the line or remain in flight. Each creature in that line must make a DC 20 Dexterity saving throw taking 20 (3d6 + 10) bludgeoning damage plus 11 (2d10) fire damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.

### Armageddon (Recharge 6)

Gogmazios lands (if it is flying) before releasing a continuous gout of flame at the ground in a 90-foot radius around it until the start of its next turn. Each creature that starts its turn or enters the area for the first time on a turn takes 11 (3d10) fire damage and an extra 5 (1d10) fire damage for every 5 feet it travels. When a creature ends its turn in the flames, it takes 110 (20d10) fire damage.

A creature wielding a shield can use its action to raise it, creating safety behind it, and reducing the damage it takes from ending its turn in the flames by 88 (16d10). Until the start of its next turn the gogmazios can only use its legendary action to increase the radius of this gout of flame by 10 feet.

At the start of the gogmazios' next turn, each creature (except ones that raised a shield) still in the flames gains one level of exhaustion from extreme heat.
