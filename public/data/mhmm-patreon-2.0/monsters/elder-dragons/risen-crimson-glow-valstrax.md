# Risen Crimson Glow Valstrax

## Bio

A risen crimson glow valstrax is a crimson glow valstrax that has overcome being afflicted by the qurio and has become more powerful as a result. It has a challenge rating of 24 (62,000 XP). It has the same statistics as a crimson glow valstrax except for the following changes:

## Stat Block

- **Challenge:** 24

## Traits

### Risen State (Recharge 4-6)

At the start of its turn (winning all ties) the crimson glow valstrax enters a Risen State until the start of its next turn, if available. While in the Risen State, some of the crimson glow valstrax's actions and traits are enhanced (as described below) and it can use its Risen actions as an action or legendary action. This Recharge trait is immune to the effects of the Elderseal material.


## Actions

### Crimson Beam

The valstrax fires a second beam in a different direction.

### Multiattack

The valstrax can make three wing attacks in place of its normal multiattack.


## Legendary Actions

### Jet Uppercut (Costs 1 Actions if used as a Legendary Action)

The crimson glow valstrax makes a Wing attack against a prone creature. If the attack hits, the target takes an extra 27 (6d8) necrotic damage.

### Risen Twister (Costs 2 Actions if used as a Legendary Action)

Risen Crimson Glow Valstrax charges up then spins around like a blender, with its wings acting like blades. Each creature in a 20-foot-radius, 40-foot-high cylinder centered on the valstrax must make a DC 21 Dexterity saving throw, taking 24 (4d8 + 6) slashing damage plus 9 (2d8) necrotic damage and be pushed up to 10 feet away and knocked prone on a failed save, or half as much damage be pushed 10 feet away and isn't knocked prone on a successful one. Stellar Shower Ambush (Recharges after a Long Rest; Cannot be used as a Legendary Action). The crimson glow valstrax flies 1000 feet into the air without provoking opportunity attacks before showering the area below it with draconic orbs. Each creature within 60 feet of the space the valstrax left must make a DC 20 Dexterity saving throw, taking 54 (12d8) necrotic damage and be afflicted with dragonblight for 1 minute on a failed save, or half as much damage and isn't afflicted with dragonblight on a successful one. On its subsequent turn, the valstrax must use its action to slam into the ground in a space within 300 feet of its starting location.
