# Jyuratodus

## Bio

Jyuratodus is related to Lavasioth, and as such resembles a bipedal coelacanth fish. Jyuratodus's scales are vulnerable to extreme heat, becoming brittle and weak when left to dry. If jyuratodus becomes too hot, it'll have a hard time breathing through its skin as well. To stay moist and keep cool, jyuratodus is always covering its body in mud to protect itself from the harsh sun, which is the main reason why it lives in swampy environments.

Adorning its body are many fins that are primarily used for balance. Although these appendages assist it as it swims, jyuratodus will turn and adjust them while walking on land, aiding it as it moves over the uneven ground. Inside of its mouth are bone-crunching teeth used for crushing and tearing into prey.

Once it has slain its victim, it'll feed the internal organs of its quarry. While swimming, jyuratodus will swallow some mud and combine it with water from its water sac, making thick globs of mud that restrict the movements of predators and prey alike. Jyuratodus are highly territorial, attacking all monsters that invade its turf.

It spends most of its time hidden away in its swamp, soaking in mud to stay moist, before hunting later in the day. While in its territory, it'll leave behind tracks in the water and on rocks as a warning to intruders, as well as kick up mud to scare off rivals. Barroth will unintentionally destroy these perimeters as it moves through the marsh, causing jyuratodus to attack the Brute Wyvern because it sees that as a challenge for its turf.

Jyuratodus only ever breed during the wet season in the Wildspire Waste. Males and females will gather together in the swamps to spawn, but only a handful of their eggs will hatch. By the time the wet season is over, most of the water will have evaporated in the swamp, drying out the eggs.

Once the young hatch, they'll search for a large monster and enter into the body of the organism by swimming into an opening (Wound, nostrils, mouth, etc.) before attaching their spine-like gills to its insides. The young jyuratodus will sneak into the bodies of larger creatures and feed on their insides until they get older.

## Traits

### Limited Amphibiousness

The jyuratodus can breathe air and water, but it needs to be submerged at least once every 4 hours to avoid suffocating.


## Actions

### Multiattack

The jyuratodus makes two Mud Ball attacks; or one Bite attack and one Tail attack. It can replace one of the melee weapon attacks with a Hip Check attack while on land.

### Bite

Melee Weapon Attack: +9 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 15 (3d6 + 5) piercing damage.

### Hip Check

Melee Weapon Attack: +9 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 24 (3d12 + 5) bludgeoning damage.

### Mud Ball

Ranged Weapon Attack: +9 to hit, range 60/240 ft., one target. Hit: 15 (3d6 + 5) bludgeoning damage.

### Tail

Melee Weapon Attack: +9 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 18 (3d8 + 5) bludgeoning damage.

### Constricting Ambush (Recharge 5-6)

The jyuratodus dives underwater before emerging underneath a target within 60 feet of it. That target must make a DC 17 Dexterity saving throw, taking 49 (9d10) bludgeoning damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one. If the target is Large or smaller, it is grappled (escape DC 15) Until this grapple ends, the target is restrained, takes 3 (1d6) bludgeoning damage at the start of each of the jyuratodus's turns, and the jyuratodus can't use its Constricting Ambush against another target.


## Bonus Actions

### Wallow (3/day)

The jyuratodus wallows in mud, covering itself in a thick layer, granting it a +3 bonus to its AC for 1 minute or until it takes 25 damage in a single turn.
