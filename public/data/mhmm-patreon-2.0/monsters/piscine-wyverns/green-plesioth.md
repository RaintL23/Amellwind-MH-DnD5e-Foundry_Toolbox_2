# Green Plesioth

## Bio

The subspecies of plesioth, a green plesioth is identical in body structure to the common species. It differs greatly in coloration however, featuring deep green scales along with white and purple striped spines and fins. Much like their relatives, the green plesioth are top predators, especially in water.

They often catch and eat aptonoth, epioth, sharqs, sea birds, frogs, and crocodiles. In the New World, these creatures must live alongside other large aquatic predators all of which are leviathans in flooded forests and deserted islands. Green plesioth has many of the same adaptations of plesioth, however, there is a few differences between the two.

The biggest difference between the two is their hunting style. Both of them are said to have the opposite hunting style. While plesioth prefer to hunt in the water, green plesioth prefer to hunt on land. Like normal plesioth, the green plesioth love frogs, which can be used to fish them out of the water.

### Tail



### Melee Weapon Attack

+9 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 17 (3d8 + 4) bludgeoning damage.

### Fling

One Medium or smaller object held or creature grappled by the plesioth is thrown up to 60 feet in a random direction and knocked prone. If a thrown target strikes a solid surface, the target takes 3 (1d6) bludgeoning damage for every 10 feet it was thrown. If the target is thrown at another creature, that creature must succeed on a DC 17 Dexterity saving throw or take the same damage and be knocked prone.

### Water Jet (Recharge 5-6)

The plesioth releases a high pressure stream of water in a 60-foot line that is 5 feet wide or in a 30-foot cone. Each creature in that area must make a DC 17 Dexterity saving throw, taking 45 (10d8) acid damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.


## Bonus Actions

### Countershading

While in water, the plesioth takes the Hide action.


## Legendary Actions

The plesioth can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action option can be used at a time and only at the end of another creature's turn. The plesioth regains spent legendary actions at the start of its turn.

### Attack

The plesioth makes one Hip Check attack.

### Throw (Costs 2 Actions)

The plesioth uses Fling.

### Slither (Costs 2 Actions)

The plesioth moves up to its speed on its belly. During this move, it can move through the spaces of other creatures without provoking opportunity attacks. Each creature the plesioth moves through must succeed on a DC 17 Dexterity saving throw or take 17 (3d8 + 4) bludgeoning damage and be pushed up to 10 feet away and knocked prone on a failed save, or half as much damage and is knocked prone.
