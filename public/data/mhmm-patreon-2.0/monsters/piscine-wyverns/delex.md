# Delex

## Bio

Delex have the ability to "swim" through sand, like the cephalos, and even expel small sand blasts from their mouth at attackers. Due to their nimbleness, they are difficult to hit. Several appear in the fights with Jhen Mohran and Dah'ren Mohran, jumping onto the boat and attacking Hunters that get in their way.

Delex hunt in small groups of six or seven individuals and likely prey upon small animals. They also scavenge off the remains left by larger desert-dwelling monsters.
