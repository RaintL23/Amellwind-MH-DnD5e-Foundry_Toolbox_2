## Traits

### Amphibious

The sleeptoad can breathe air and water.

### Death Burst

The sleeptoad explodes when it drops to 0 hit points. Each creature within 10 feet of it must succeed on a DC 11 Constitution saving throw or fall unconscious for 1 minute, until the sleeper takes damage, or someone uses an action to shake or slap the sleeper awake.

### Standing Leap

The sleeptoad's long jump is up to 10 feet and its high jump is up to 5 feet, with or without a running start.


## Actions

### Bite

Melee Weapon Attack: +4 to hit, Reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 7 (1d10 + 2) piercing damage plus 5 (1d10) poison damage, and the target must succeed on a DC 11 Constitution saving throw or be incapacitated until the end of the sleeptoad's next turn.
