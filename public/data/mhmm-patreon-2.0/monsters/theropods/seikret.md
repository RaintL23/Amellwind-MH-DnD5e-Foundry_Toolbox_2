# Seikret

## Bio

The seikret is a small, feathered bird wyvern domesticated as a riding animal. Adults are typically, green and raptor-like, and their plumage can shift in hue with diet. Its forelimbs unfurl into short wings that enable controlled glides across broken terrain. Handlers prize the species for keen scent-tracking and reliable route-finding; trained individuals respond to calls, locate their riders, and bound over obstacles before gliding to safer ground.

## Traits

### Glide

When the seikret falls and isn't incapacitated, it can subtract up to 100 feet from the fall when calculating falling damage, and it can move up to 2 feet horizontally for every 1 foot it descends.
