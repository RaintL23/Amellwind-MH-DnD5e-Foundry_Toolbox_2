# Kranodath

## Bio

The kranodath is a theropod with ash-gray scales that shade to violet along the neck and a blunt red nose horn. Bony plates on the head and neck can spread outward, turning charges into heavy headbutts. It hunts in small packs across arid basins and rocky scrub, rushing prey with short leaps and clinging to larger targets to batter the skull and stun them.

Packs keep tight around carcasses and defend them with pecks and ramming rushes. still shrouded in feathers, it must make a DC 11 Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, the target falls unconscious until the end of its next turn, until the sleeper takes damage, or until a creature uses an action to shake or slap the sleeper awake.
