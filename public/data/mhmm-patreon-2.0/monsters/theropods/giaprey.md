# Giaprey

## Bio

Giaprey have crystal blue scales with sky blue stripes. Unlike its other relatives, the Giaprey are able to survive the freezing temperatures of snowy mountains and icy tundra where they thrive. This is because of its skin being warmer than its blood, reducing heat loss and repelling ambient chill.

Giaprey live in packs and obey their leader the Giadrome in what it dictates to the pack. Though they are small, they are very aggressive when someone enters their territory.
