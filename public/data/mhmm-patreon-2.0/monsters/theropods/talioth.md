# Talioth

## Bio

The Talioth is a theropod with a long, slender build, an elongated hooked beak lined with curved teeth, and a row of serrated dorsal spikes that range from tan to copper-red. It is a pack hunter that prowls open plains and rocky scrub, rushing targets in bursts and seizing them with a gripping jaw; groups attack larger beasts from several angles and can bring them down by sheer numbers.

## Traits

### Pack Tactics

The talioth has advantage on an attack roll against a creature if at least one of the talioth's allies is within 5 feet of the creature and the ally isn't incapacitated.


## Actions

### Bite

Melee Weapon Attack: +3 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 4 (1d6 + 1) piercing damage. If the talioth moved at least 20 feet straight toward a Medium or larger creature immediately before the hit, that target must succeed on a DC 12 Strength saving throw or the talioth attaches itself to the target (escape DC 12). While attached, the target's movement speed is halved and the Talioth can't use its Bite attack.

### Claw

Melee Weapon Attack: +3 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 6 (2d4 + 1) slashing damage. 560
