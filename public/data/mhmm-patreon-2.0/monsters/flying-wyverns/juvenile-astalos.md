## Traits

### Dive Attack

If the astalos is flying and dives at least 30 feet straight toward a target and then hits it with a melee weapon attack, the attack deals an extra 4 (1d8) lightning damage to the target.


## Actions

### Multiattack

The astalos makes one Horn attack, one Tail attack, and one Wing attack.

### Horn

Melee Weapon Attack: +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 8 (1d8 + 4) piercing damage plus 2 (1d4) lightning damage.

### Tail

Melee Weapon Attack: +6 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 8 (1d8 + 4) piercing damage plus 2 (1d4) lightning damage. If the target is a creature, it must make a DC 14 Constitution saving throw or be paralyzed for 1 minute. The target can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the paralysis on itself on a success.

### Wing

Melee Weapon Attack: +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 7 (1d6 + 4) piercing damage plus 2 (1d4) lightning damage.

### Lightning Pillar (Recharge 5-6)

A vertical column of lightning roars down from the sky in a location within 60 feet of the astalos. Each creature in a 10-footradius, 40-foot-high cylinder centered on that location must make a DC 13 Dexterity saving throw, taking 21 (6d6) lightning damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one. 333
