# Diablos Whelp

## Bio

The diablos whelp is a small, heavily plated flying wyvern with short, blunt horns and a sturdy build. Its armor is already thick for its size, and its limbs and tail are welldeveloped. The horns are used for ramming and shoveling sand, while its burrowing ability allows it to move quickly beneath the surface.

Diablos whelps are herbivores that feed primarily on desert cacti. Despite this diet, they are highly territorial and will attack any intruder that enters their feeding grounds. They rely on sudden horn charges, tail strikes, and a high-pitched screech that can disorient nearby creatures before closing in.

While not as powerful as adults, they are aggressive and persistent, often fighting until the threat retreats. Diablos whelps are born in shallow burrows dug by the mother. The mother will guard the nest in the early months, driving off any predator or rival wyverns. Once the whelp is strong enough to dig and defend itself, the mother will drive it from their territory, forcing it to establish its own feeding grounds.

This dispersal reduces competition for food and helps prevent fights between related diablos. Diablos whelps are found in arid, open regions with loose sand suitable for burrowing. They stay close to feeding areas and will rarely stray far from them, emerging from underground to patrol or chase off larger monsters and hunters.
