# Bazelgeusling

## Bio

A bazelgeusling is a young, adolescent Bazelgeuse, bearing the same bronzy sheen and thick, heavy scales as its adult counterparts, albeit on a smaller, less developed frame. Despite its size, the bazelgeusling is already a formidable presence, showcasing the characteristic explosive scales that have begun to form on its underside.

These scales, while not as potent as those of an adult, still possess enough power to cause significant damage and disruption. The bazelgeusling occasionally enters a preliminary "superheated" state, causing the gaps between its scales to glow red, signaling its readiness to shed explosive scales in defense or aggression.

Behaviorally, bazelgeuslings are nomadic predators, much like their fully-grown kin. They can be found wandering various environments, learning to assert themselves within local food chains. While not yet apex predators, they exhibit a strong desire for combat, frequently engaging in skirmishes with other monsters to hone their skills and establish dominance.

## Traits

### Carpet Bomb

For every 20 feet the Bazelgeusling flies, it dislodges an explosive scale which lands in an unoccupied space directly below it.

### Explosive Scales

The Bazelgeusling scales, when dislodged from its body, act as a time bomb. On initiative count 20 (losing all initiative ties) all scales explode dealing 7 (2d6) fire damage to each creature in a 5-foot radius of them.

### Shed

Whenever the Bazelgeusling makes a melee weapon attack it dislodges an explosive scale which lands in an unoccupied space with 5 feet of the Bazelgeusling.

### Wounded Fury

While the Bazelgeusling has 34 hit points or fewer, it enters a "superheated" state. While superheated it has advantage on attack rolls and its Explosive Scales detonate at the end of its turn.


## Actions

### Multiattack

The Bazelgeusling makes one Headbutt attack, one Tail attack, and one Wing attack.

### Headbutt

Melee Weapon Attack: +9 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 14 (2d8 + 5) bludgeoning damage. If the Bazelgeusling is flying and dives at least 30 feet straight toward a target immediately before the hit, the target takes an extra 4 (1d8) bludgeoning damage.

### Tail

Melee Weapon Attack: +9 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 12 (2d6 + 5) bludgeoning damage.

### Wing

Melee Weapon Attack: +9 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 16 (2d10 + 5) bludgeoning damage.

### Fire Breath (Recharge 5-6)
