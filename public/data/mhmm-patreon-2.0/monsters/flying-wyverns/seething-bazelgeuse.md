# Seething Bazelgeuse

## Bio

The seething bazelgeuse is an even more dominant predator than its regular cousins. This Variant species can defend its territory against formidable intruders like glavenus, brachydios, and tigrex. Such monsters avoid fighting it unless necessary, as battles with seething bazelgeuse are extremely dangerous and likely to cause serious injury.

Seething bazelgeuse are fierce competitors for savage deviljho in the ecosystem, with both species boasting tremendous power. Clashes between them are some of the most destructive in the natural world. Elder Dragons, which are not uncommon in the Recess, also pose a significant threat to seething bazelgeuse.

Seething bazelgeuse are more dangerous due to their advanced explosive scales, enhanced by a substance called "Distilled Blast Fluid." This fluid makes their bomb scales more combustive the longer the creature is aggravated, peaking when the scales emit a fiery purple glow and produce large, powerful explosions.

The scales become sticky in this state, latching onto surfaces before exploding. However, this mechanism has limits, and seething bazelgeuse must periodically "reset" after generating supercharged scales for a while. Powerful blows to their scale-making organ can also sabotage this process.

Behaviorally, seething bazelgeuse are similar to their regular cousins but are more accustomed to direct ground combat, making them tougher foes. They demonstrate improved tactical thinking, often throwing scales around themselves to limit their enemies' safe zones, complicating maneuvering around their attacks.

This tactic also serves as a deterrent against distant attackers, a notable weakness of the normal species.

## Traits

### Carpet Bomb

For every 20 feet the bazelgeuse flies, it dislodges an explosive scale which lands in an unoccupied space directly below it.

### Explosive Scales

The bazelgeuse scales, when dislodged from its body, act as a time bomb. On initiative count 20 (losing all initiative ties) all scales explode dealing 14 (4d6) fire damage to all creatures in a 10-foot radius of them.

### Shed

Whenever the bazelgeuse makes a melee weapon attack it dislodges an explosive scale which lands in an unoccupied space with 5 feet of its location.

### Wounded Fury

While the bazelgeuse has 100 hit points or fewer, it enters a "superheated" state gaining advantage on attack rolls. In addition, Explosive Scales grow, increasing its explosion radius by 5 feet and deal an extra 1d6 fire damage.


## Actions

### Multiattack

The bazelgeuse makes one Headbutt attack, one Tail attack, and one Wing attack.

### Headbutt

Melee Weapon Attack: +13 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 19 (3d8 + 6) bludgeoning damage. If the bazelgeuse is flying and dives at least 30 feet straight toward a target immediately before the hit, the target takes an extra 4 (1d8) bludgeoning damage.

### Tail

Melee Weapon Attack: +13 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 16 (3d6 + 6) bludgeoning damage.

### Wing

Melee Weapon Attack: +13 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 22 (3d10 + 6) bludgeoning damage.

### No Man's Landing (Recharge 6)

The bazelgeuse soars 30 feet into the sky while scattering 15 explosive scales in a 60-foot radius around it, in a space that is at least 15 feet apart from another scale. It then comes crashing back down to the ground in a space within 60 feet of it, causing an explosion in a 20-foot radius around it. Each scale in the explosions area is destroyed, and each creature in that area must make a DC 22 Dexterity saving throw, taking 63 (14d8) fire damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.


## Legendary Actions

The bazelgeuse can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action option can be used at a time and only at the end of another creature's turn. The bazelgeuse regains spent legendary actions at the start of its turn.

### Fling

The bazelgeuse dislodges 3 explosive scales and sends them flying with its tail. Choose a location within 60 feet of the bazelgeuse, each scale lands in an unoccupied space within 15 feet of that location.

### Fire Breath (Costs 3 Actions)

The bazelgeuse exhales fire in a 60-foot cone. Each creature in that area must make a DC 22 Dexterity saving throw, taking 46 (7d12) fire damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.
