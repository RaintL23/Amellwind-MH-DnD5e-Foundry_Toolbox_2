# Dreadqueen Rathian

## Bio

The dreadqueen rathian also known as the “Purple Poison Princess” is a larger, venom-suffused matriarch whose scales show a faint violet sheen. Her tail carries detachable toxic spines that create lingering poison clouds when scattered and her fire attacks hit harder than the a standard rathian.

She is highly territorial and attacks on sight using tail flips to scatter spines, diving tail slams, sweeping fire breath, and short aerial swoops. The dreadqueen is highly adaptable; she ranges across many biomes including: temperate forests and hills, active volcanic zones, open steppes, dense jungles, primeval woodlands, rocky coastal islands, ancient ruins, and mountaintop crags.

In newer frontiers, sightings are common on remote tropical islands.

## Traits

### Deadly Poison

Poison damage dealt by the rathian bypasses a creature's resistance to the damage and half damage to a creature immune to poison damage.

### Flyby

The rathian doesn't provoke an opportunity attack when she flies out of an enemy's reach.

### Legendary Resistance (3/Day)

If the rathian fails a saving throw, she can choose to succeed instead.

### Poisonous Spike Scatter

When the rathian makes a tail attack, it leaves a spike that expels poisonous gas for 1 minute in an unoccupied space adjacent to the target. When a creature ends its turn in a space adjacent to a spike, it must succeed on a DC 22 Constitution saving throw or be poisoned as described in the rathian's Tail attack.

A creature can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success. If a creature's saving throw is successful or the effect ends for it, the creature is immune to the rathian's Frightful Presence for the next 24 hours.

The rathian exhales a fireball to a point within 120 feet of it. Each creature in a 30foot radius sphere centered on that point must make a DC 22 Dexterity saving throw, taking 101 (29d6) fire damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.
