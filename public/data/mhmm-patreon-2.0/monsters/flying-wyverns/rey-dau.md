# Rey Dau

## Bio

The rey dau is a large bipedal flying wyvern with bony golden scales. Black scales cover the belly, joints, and wing roots. Its wings form a single membrane and each wing bears a rigid blade-like ridge used as a weapon. Its defining feature are the large horns spread out on each side of its head which can be folded inwards and used to channel electricity akin to a rail gun.

After harnessing electricity, its wing blades and back spikes glow bright blue with fulgurite. The rey dau functions as an apex predator in open, windy biomes. It appears most often during thunderstorms, drawing in ambient charge and discharging focused bolts from its folded horns.

After a strike, sand vitrifies on the wings as fulgurite. The hardened glass sharpens its slashes. It uses diving passes, tight rolls, and lateral cuts with the wing blades. It can deliver short-range thrusts by charging the horns as a spear. They rey dau is highly territorial and attacks visible intruders on sight.

Nearby settlements hang fulgurite chimes that mimic wing scrape to deter lesser monsters. The species can recover from fatigue after a natural lightning hit. Prey includes large herbivores and smaller wyverns. Few rivals challenge a mature individual. Rey dau are found in arid plains, wind-cut canyons, and adjacent grasslands.

It makes its lair on narrow ridges and chasms. Sites with frequent storms and silica-rich sand are preferred. Its activity peaks during seasonal storms.

## Traits

### Storm Bringer

A 1-mile wide thunderstorm follows the Rey Dau, lightly obscuring the area with rain. At the start of each of its turns, lightning strikes one creature in the area, and there is a 50% chance that strong winds (greater than 10 miles per hour) blow through the 1-mile area, that last until the end of its turn. A creature struck by lightning must succeed on a DC 19 Dexterity saving throw or take 11 (2d10) lightning damage and be afflicted with thunderblight for 1 minute. A thunderblighted creature can make a DC 19 Constitution the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success.

The rey dau can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action option can be used at a time and only at the end of another creature's turn. The rey dau regains spent legendary actions at the start of its turn.

### Attack (1/round)

The rey dau makes one Horn attack, or one Lightning Jolt attack.

### Raking Lightning Sweep (Costs 2 Actions)

While on the ground, the rey dau flies 10 feet into the air, then dragging one of its massive wings along the ground in a 20-foot line that is 5 feet wide and 10-feet tall. It can land at the end of the line or remain in flight. Each creature in that line must make a DC 19 Dexterity saving throw taking 16 (3d6 + 6) bludgeoning damage plus 11 (2d10) lightning damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.

The rey dau sweeps its tail back and forth in a 10-foot line that is 15 feet wide. Each creature in the area must make a DC 19 Dexterity saving throw.
