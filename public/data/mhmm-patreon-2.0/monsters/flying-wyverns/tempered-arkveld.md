## Traits

### Empowered (Mythic Trait; Recharges after a Short or Long Rest)

If the arkveld would be reduced to 0 hit points, its current hit point total instead resets to 310 (27d12 + 135) hit points, it recharges its Charged Dragon Burst, and it regains any expended uses of Legendary Resistance. Additionally, the some of the arkveld's actions are enhanced and it can use the options in the "Mythic Actions" section for 1 hour.

### Legendary Resistance (3/Day)

If the arkveld fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.

### Magic Resistance

The arkveld has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.


## Actions

### Multiattack

The arkveld makes two Chain attacks.

### Melee Weapon Attack

+13 to hit, reach 15 ft., one target. Hit: 24 (4d8 + 6) bludgeoning damage plus 14 (4d6) necrotic damage. If the target is a creature, the arkveld can choose one of the following additional effects: The target is grappled (escape DC 23). Until this grapple ends the target is restrained. If the target is Huge or larger, the arkveld can't make Chain attacks against another target. The arkveld moves 10 feet away from the target without provoking opportunity attacks. The arkveld has advantage on its next chain attack against a creature adjacent to the target on this turn.

### Tail



### Melee Weapon Attack

+13 to hit, 10 ft., one target. Hit: 22 (3d10 + 6) bludgeoning damage. If the target is a creature, it must succeed on a DC 21 Strength saving throw or be pushed 10 feet away and knocked prone.

### Charged Dragon Burst (Recharge 5-6)

The arkveld slams both sets of its red glowing chains on the ground creating a wave of draconic explosions in a 45-foot line that is 15 feet wide. Each creature in that line must make a DC 21 Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save, the creature takes 93 (17d10) necrotic damage and is afflicted with dragonblight for 1 minute, or until cured by the lesser restoration spell or a similar magical effect or item. On a successful save, the creature takes half as much damage and isn't afflicted with dragonblight.


## Legendary Actions

The arkveld can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action option can be used at a time and only at the end of another creature's turn. The arkveld regains spent legendary actions at the start of its turn.

### Attack

The arkveld makes one Tail attack.

### Fling

One Large or smaller object held, or creature grappled by the arkveld is thrown up to 60 feet in a random direction (or 90 feet if its Empowered trait activated in the last hour) and knocked prone. If a thrown target strikes a solid surface, the target takes 3 (1d6) bludgeoning damage for every 10 feet it was thrown. If the target is thrown at another creature, that creature must succeed on a DC 21 Dexterity saving throw or take the same damage and be knocked prone.

### Flying Sweep (Costs 2 Actions)

The arkveld flies up to half its speed and makes one Chain attack as it flies or when it lands. If the arkveld's Empowered state activated in the last hour, it can instead make two Chain attacks, each against a different target.

### Dragon Burst (Costs 2 Actions)

The arkveld slams the chains on one arm into the ground releasing draconic energy in a 30-foot line that is 5 feet wide (10-feet wide if the its Empowered trait activated in the last hour). Each creature in that line must make a DC 21 Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save, the creature takes 22 (4d10) necrotic damage and is afflicted with dragonblight for 1 minute, or until cured by the lesser restoration spell or a similar magical effect or item.
