## Traits

### After Shock

When the rey dau hits a creature that is on the ground with a weapon attack, each creature within 5 feet of the target must succeed on a DC 20 Dexterity saving throw or take 11 (2d10) lightning damage and be afflicted with thunderblight for 1 minute. A thunderblighted creature can make a DC 20 Constitution the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success.

### Legendary Resistance (3/Day)

If the rey dau fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.

### Magic Resistance

The rey dau has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

### Storm Bringer

A 1-mile wide thunderstorm follows the Rey Dau, lightly obscuring the area with rain. At the start of each of its turns, lightning strikes one creature in the area, and there is a 50% chance that strong winds (greater than 10 miles per hour) blow through the 1-mile area, that last until the end of its turn. A creature struck by lightning must succeed on a DC 20 Dexterity saving throw or take 11 (2d10) lightning damage and be afflicted with thunderblight for 1 minute. A thunderblighted creature can make a DC 20 Constitution the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success.

## Legendary Actions

The rey dau can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action option can be used at a time and only at the end of another creature's turn. The rey dau regains spent legendary actions at the start of its turn.

### Attack (1/round)

The rey dau makes one Horn attack, or one Lightning Jolt attack.

### Raking Lightning Sweep (Costs 2 Actions)

While on the ground, the rey dau flies 10 feet into the air, then dragging one of its massive wings along the ground in a 20-foot line that is 5 feet wide and 10-feet tall. It can land at the end of the line or remain in flight. Each creature in that line must make a DC 20 Dexterity saving throw taking 16 (3d6 + 6) bludgeoning damage plus 11 (2d10) lightning damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.

### Tail Sweep (Costs 2 Actions)

The rey dau sweeps its tail back and forth in a 10-foot line that is 15 feet wide. Each creature in the area must make a DC 20 Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save the creature takes 22 (3d10 + 6) bludgeoning damage and is knocked prone. On a successful save, it takes half as much damage and isn't knocked prone.

The rey dau fires a concentrated beam of lightning at the ground in front of it. Each creature in a 15-foot cube originating from the rey dau must make a DC 20 Dexterity saving throw.
