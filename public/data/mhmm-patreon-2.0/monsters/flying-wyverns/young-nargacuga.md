## Traits

### Pounce

If the nargacuga moves at least 20 feet straight toward a creature and then hits it with a bladed wings attack on the same turn, that target must succeed on a DC 15 Strength saving throw or be knocked prone. If the target is prone, the nargacuga can make one bite attack against it as a bonus action.


## Actions

### Multiattack

The nargacuga makes two attacks: one with its bladed wings and one with its tail.

### Bite

Melee Weapon Attack: +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 10 (2d6 + 3) piercing damage.

### Bladed Wings

Melee Weapon Attack: +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 10 (2d6 + 3) slashing damage.

### Tail

Melee Weapon Attack: +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 14 (2d10 + 3) bludgeoning damage.


## Bonus Actions

### Shadow Stealth

While in dim light or darkness, the nargacuga takes the Hide action.
