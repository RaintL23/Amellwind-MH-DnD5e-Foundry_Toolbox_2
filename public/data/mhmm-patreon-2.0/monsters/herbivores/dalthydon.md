# Dalthydon

## Bio

The dalthydon is a small, bipedal herbivore with white-andbrown fur, and a reinforced head capped by short, ram-like horns. It moves in herds that shift between habitats as conditions change and is commonly kept as livestock due to its docile nature. When threatened, the herd forms a defensive ring and individuals drive predators back with headlong rams.

It is most often found in open plains and forest margins.
