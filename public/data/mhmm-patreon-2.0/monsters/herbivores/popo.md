# Popo

## Bio

A large grazing herbivore, Popo is somewhat easy prey for anything larger than itself. Its size and tusks only work to deter smaller predators. Popo are very docile creatures and will run when one of their members in a group is under attack or when a threat enters their area. They will also group together when a large monster is near, like the Aptonoth in more temperate climates.

## Traits

### Trampling Charge

If the popo moved at least 20 feet straight toward a creature and then hits it with a Gore attack on the same turn, that target must succeed on a DC 10 Strength saving throw or be knocked prone. If the target is prone, the popo can make one Stomp attack against it as a bonus action.


## Actions

### Multiattack

The popo makes one Gore attack and one Stomp attack.

### Gore

Melee Weapon Attack: +3 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 4 (1d6 + 1) piercing damage.

### Stomp

Melee Weapon Attack: +3 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 3 (1d4 + 1) bludgeoning damage.
