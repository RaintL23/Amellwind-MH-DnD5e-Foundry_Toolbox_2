# Rhenoplos

## Bio

Rhenoplos is a quadrupedal, Ceratopsian-like monster with an armored body. Its face features a small nose horn and is surrounded by a frill. While the eyes of Rhenoplos are not particularly proficient, their hearing is. Rhenoplos charge blindly at whatever sound startles them, including large monsters.

However, this makes them easy to dodge and easy prey for predators placing them near the bottom of the food chain with most other herbivores. These creatures live in small groups, spending most of their days traversing the hot environments while foraging for shrubs and other plant matter.
