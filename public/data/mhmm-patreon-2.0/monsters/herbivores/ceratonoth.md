# Ceratonoth

## Bio

The ceratonoth is a small herbivorous quadruped with yellow-brown, scales reminiscent of a pangolin, a long snout, and a plated tail; males are darker and carry three prominent dorsal spikes. Herds roam open deserts and savannas and gravitate to storm-prone flats, retreating to sheltered ravines or caves when weather turns severe.

During sandstorms, males use their dorsal spikes to draw lightning and discharge it into the ground, sometimes leaving fulgurite on their plates, and they quickly steer the herd away from large predators.
