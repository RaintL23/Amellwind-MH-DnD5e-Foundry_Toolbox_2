# Larinoth

## Bio

Larinoth are quite huge monsters, compared to other herbivores and even many large monsters. It possesses a great height, which is mostly attributed to its extremely lengthy neck. The tail of a Larinoth is rounded and club-like, with spiked plating that runs along the back and up to the crest on its head.

Said plating is nearly entirely green, and the Larinoth's underbelly is a cream color. Raw Meat Provides 2 days rations when cooked. White Liver A white-colored liver, popular for its juicy texture. Sells for 100 gp.
