# Copper Blangonga

## Bio

Copper blangonga's main difference between normal blangongas is its short, brown pelt, which would normally be a snow white coloration. Possibly helps the creature to retain heat in the cold night and to release it in the hot day and can also serve as camouflage in the rocky parts of the desert.

The fur is also remarkably compact, probably to fend off surprise attacks from genpreys and other predators. Its facial features change, sporting longer tusks able to cut through the hard shell of apceros. Instead of spewing snow, the copper blangonga spews sand. Incredibly powerful, they can carry large boulders and throw them at enemies or prey, dealing massive damage.

They also manage to dig through earth and sand, perfect for ambushing prey. Its hindlegs are notably hard and developed, as it needs to jump great distances to travel around the vast desert. Due to the lack of smaller game in the desert and competition with other monsters, the copper blangonga appears to have evolved into a solitary creature, having no pack of blango followers.

Like their mountain-dwelling relatives copper blangonga are powerful predators high in the food chain. Due to these Blangonga being rare, it is hard to say what it feeds on. In their desert environments these large primates compete with equally fierce predators.

## Traits

### Desert Camouflage

The blangonga has advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks made to hide in desert terrain.

### Frost Susceptibility

If the blangonga takes cold damage, it suffers several effects until the end of its next turn: its speed is halved, it takes a −2 penalty to AC and Dexterity saving throws, and it can't use reaction.

### Standing Leap

The blangonga's long jump is up to 30 feet and its high jump is up to 15 feet, without a running start. In addition, the blangonga does not provoke opportunity attacks while moving with a jump.

### Tunneler

The blangonga can burrow through solid rock at half its burrow speed and leaves a 10-foot-diameter tunnel in its wake.


## Actions

### Multiattack

The blangonga makes three Fist attacks. It can use its Boulder attack in place of any melee attack.

### Bite

Melee Weapon Attack: +10 to hit, reach 5 ft., a creature that is grappled by the blangonga. Hit: 23 (4d8 + 5) piercing damage.

### Fist

Melee Weapon Attack: +10 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 14 (2d8 + 5) bludgeoning damage. If the target is a Medium or smaller creature, it is grappled (escape DC 20). Until this grapple ends the creature is restrained, and the blangonga can't grapple another target.

### Boulder

Ranged Weapon Attack: +10 to hit, reach 60/240 ft., one target. Hit: 18 (3d8 +5) bludgeoning damage. If the target is a creature, it must succeed on a DC 18 Strength saving throw or be knocked prone.

### Earthrending Slam (Recharge 5-6)

The blangonga slams its fists into the ground, creating a powerful shockwave that fractures the earth in a 20-foot square area originating from it. Each creature within that area must make a DC 18 Strength saving throw. On a failed save, a creature takes 35 (10d6) bludgeoning damage and is knocked prone. On a successful save, a creature takes half as much damage and isn’t knocked prone. If the saving throw fails by 5 or more, the target is also stunned until the end of its next turn.


## Reactions

### Retreat

When the blangonga is hit by a melee attack, it can leap up to 20 feet away from the attacker.


## Legendary Actions

The blangonga can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action option can be used at a time and only at the end of another creature's turn. The blangonga regains spent legendary actions at the start of its turn.

### Nibble

The blangonga makes one Bite attack against a creature it is grappling.

### Quake (Costs 2 Actions)

The blangonga reaches for the sky and slams its body on the ground. Each creature in contact with the ground within 15 feet of the blangonga must succeed on a DC 18 Strength saving throw or be knocked prone. If the saving throw fails by 5 or more, the target is also incapacitated until the end of its next turn.

### Sand Blast (Costs 2 Actions)

The blangonga exhales sand in a 30-foot cone. Each creature in that area must make a DC 17 Dexterity saving throw, taking 22 (5d8) bludgeoning damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.
