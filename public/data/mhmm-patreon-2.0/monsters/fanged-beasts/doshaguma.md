# Doshaguma

## Bio

The doshuguma has two carves. Use 1-10 on the Alpha table for the normal doshaguma loot. The doshaguma is a large monster with a bear-like gait. It has thick golden-brown fur. The back of the skull and the peak of the spine are shaggier and form a mane-like ridge. Like arzuros, it has armored forelegs and bear-like paws and claws.

The armor is lighter, with small scutes and bumps. The face is wrinkled, reddish, and hairless. Two large oval bony pads sit on the crown. The eyes are small relative to most monsters. It bears tusk-like canines on both jaws. The lower tusks are slightly larger and project upward.

The tail is long and covered in flowing brown fur. Doshaguma are aggressive and territorial. They occupy a wide range of habitats. They form small packs that attack as a group, usually led by a single alpha with two subordinates. The cause is unknown. A common view is that grouping allows larger prey to be tackled.

Pack cohesion is weak. Individuals often shadow one another to share kills rather than coordinate closely. They stand on the hind legs and roar to appear larger and more threatening. Like odogaron, they often engage any rival regardless of size or strength. In combat they rely on brute strength.

They swipe, slash, and smash with the forelimbs. They bite with enlarged canines. They also charge with jaws open. They can lift and throw carcasses,often spikeless ceratonoths.

### Claw

Melee Weapon Attack: +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 5 (1d6 + 2) slashing damage.

### Headbutt

Melee Weapon Attack: +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 11 (2d8 + 2) bludgeoning damage. If the doshaguma moved at least 10 feet straight toward the target immediately before the hit, the target takes an extra 4 (1d8) bludgeoning damage. If the target is a creature, it must succeed on a DC 12 Strength saving throw or be pushed up to 10 feet away and knocked prone.

### Smash (Recharge 5-6)

The doshaguma performs a powerful claw attack against one creature within 5 feet of it. That creature must make a DC 12 Dexterity saving throw, taking 9 (2d6 + 2) bludgeoning damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one. If the save fails by 5 or more, the target is also blinded until it uses an action to wipe the dust from its eyes. 267
