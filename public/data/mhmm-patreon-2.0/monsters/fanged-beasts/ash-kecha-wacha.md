# Ash Kecha Wacha

## Bio

The ash kecha wacha is a more aggressive subspecies of the kecha wacha, often called the “white fox monkey” for its striking, fox-like appearance. In contrast to the bright orange pelt of its cousin, it sports washed-blue fur with black markings on its hands, feet, and head. Its large, versatile ears serve a multitude of purposes—listening for insect larvae inside trees, detecting predators, and acting as a defensive display when threatened.

If cornered, it folds its ears over its face, revealing bright eyespots meant to intimidate attackers. While this blocks its vision, the lowered position of its ears allows it to detect movements through sound alone. Compared to the regular kecha wacha, this subspecies covers its face with its ears far more often.

Its ears are tougher, capable of deflecting some attacks, and have unique adaptations. The ash kecha wacha can clap the spiked edges of its ears together to create sparks, igniting a flammable substance expelled from its trunk. This substance comes from fermented materials gained from certain fruits and insects in its diet, igniting upon contact with oxygen.

Its vivid coloration serves as both a warning to predators and an attraction to potential mates. It possesses a patagium, a membrane of skin linking its limbs to its body, enabling it to perform long, controlled glides. Unlike its tree-bound orange relative, the ash kecha wacha often ventures beyond its territory and spends less time in the canopy, favoring bold, aggressive tactics.

## Traits

### Glide

When the kecha wacha falls and isn't incapacitated, it can subtract up to 100 feet from the fall when calculating falling damage, and it can move up to 2 feet horizontally for every 1 foot it descends.

### Spider Climb

The kecha wacha can climb difficult surfaces, including upside down on ceilings, without needing to make an ability check.

### Standing Leap

The kecha wacha's long jump is up to 20feet and its high jump is up to 10 feet, without a running start.


## Actions

### Multiattack

The kecha wacha makes three Claw attacks, or three Fire Spit attacks.

### Claws

Melee Weapon Attack: +10 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 15 (2d8 + 6) slashing damage.

### Fire Spit

Ranged Weapon Attack: +10 to hit, range 30/120 ft., one target. Hit: 9 (2d8) fire damage. If the target is a creature, it ignites. Until a creature takes an action to douse the fire, the target takes 4 (1d8) fire damage at the start of each of its turns.

### Tantrum (Recharge 5-6)

The kecha wacha flails its fist around it in anger. Each creature within 10 feet of the kecha wacha must make a DC 18 Strength saving throw, taking 28 (8d6) bludgeoning damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one.


## Bonus Actions

### Earmuffs

The kecha wacha covers its face with its ear, gaining a +2 bonus to its AC until it is subjected to thunder damage or uses another bonus action to uncover its face. While the kecha wacha can hear and its face is covered, it has blindsight out to 60 feet but is blind beyond that radius.


## Legendary Actions

The kecha wacha can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action option can be used at a time and only at the end of another creature's turn. The kecha wacha regains spent legendary actions at the start of its turn.

### Claw Attack

The kecha wacha makes one Claw attack.

### Gliding Strike (Costs 2 Actions)

The kecha wacha jumps in the air without provoking opportunity attacks and glides up to 20 feet. It can then make one Claw attack.

### Roar (Costs 2 Actions)

The kecha wacha lets out a deep roar. Each creature within 10 feet of the kecha wacha must succeed on a DC 16 Constitution saving throw or be incapacitated until the end of its next turn. If the saving throw fails by 5 or more, the target is stunned instead.
