## Traits

### Pack Tactics

The kecha wacha has advantage on an attack roll against a creature if at least one of the kecha wacha's allies is within 5 feet of the creature and the ally isn't incapacitated.

### Spider Climb

The kecha wacha can climb difficult surfaces, including upside down on ceilings, without needing to make an ability check.

The kecha wacha makes two Claw attacks or two Mucus attacks.
