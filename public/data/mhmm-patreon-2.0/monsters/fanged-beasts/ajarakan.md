# Ajarakan

## Bio

The ajarakan are fanged beast that resembles a mandrill monkey in covered in golden-green scales, streaked blue and red. It has heavy forelimbs and unlike other fanged beasts it has flat, padded nails. Two short crest rows crown a bear-like head with a bright blue nose, while flame-shaped ridges line the back.

A long tail ends in jagged spines. The ajarakan are extremely territorial. It grapples with brute strength, tears up burning stones to hurl, and climbs to launch leaping strikes. By whipping its tail over its armor, it kindles the shell to red heat, adding scorching punches and ground -bursts of magma.

Its signature spin-and-dive triggers a fiery shockwave. Ajarakan are pack hunters, though apex predators can break the troop alone.

## Actions

### Multiattack

The ajarakan makes two Fist attacks or it makes two Boulder attacks.

### Fist

Melee Weapon Attack: +9 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 15 (3d6 + 5) bludgeoning damage. If the ajarakan is in the Ignited State and rolled a 20 on the attack roll, the target is grappled (escape DC 19). Until the grapple ends, the creature is restrained and takes 5 (1d10) fire damage at the start of each of its turns.

### Tail

Melee Weapon Attack: +9 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 21 (3d10 + 5) bludgeoning damage. If the target is a Large or smaller creature, it must succeed on a DC 17 Strength saving throw or be pushed 10 feet away and knocked prone.

### Boulder

Ranged Weapon Attack: +9 to hit, range 60/240 ft., one target. Hit: 21 (3d10 + 5) bludgeoning damage or fire damage while in the Ignited State. Ground pound. The ajarakan slams at a point on the ground within 10 feet of it. Each creature in a 5-foot radius centered on that point must make a DC 17 Dexterity or Strength saving throw (target's choice) or be knocked prone and takes 19 (4d6 + 5) bludgeoning damage. On a successful save, the creature takes only half the damage, isn't knocked prone, and is pushed 5 feet out of the ajarakan's space into an unoccupied space of the creature's choice. If no unoccupied spaces is within range, the creature instead falls prone in the ajarakan's space. If the ajarakan is in the Ignited State, the top layer of the ground melts into lava. A creature that starts its turn in the lava or enters it for the first time on a turn, takes 5 (1d10) fire damage.

### Thrash (Recharge 5-6)

While in the Ignited State, the ajarakan rapidly spins before jumping into the air without provoking opportunity attacks before it comes crashing down in a space that contains one or more creatures within 30 feet of it. Each of those creatures must succeed on a DC 17 Dexterity or Strength saving throw (target's choice) or take 18 (3d8 + 5) bludgeoning damage plus 16 (3d10) fire damage and be knocked prone. On a successful save, the creature takes only half the damage, isn't knocked prone, and is pushed 5 feet out of the ajarakan's space into an unoccupied space of the creature's choice. If no unoccupied spaces is within range, the creature instead falls prone in the ajarakan's space.


## Bonus Actions

### Ignited State

If the ajarakan is standing and hasn't moved yet on this turn, it can use this Bonus Action to reduces its movement to 0 until the start of its next turn as it covers its body in lava. At the start of its next turn, it enters the Ignited State for 1 minute, unless it was incapacitated or knocked prone. While in the Ignited State, the ajarakan can use its Lava Spray action and other actions are enhanced.


## Legendary Actions

The ajarakan can take 2 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action option can be used at a time and only at the end of another creature's turn. The ajarakan regains spent legendary actions at the start of its turn.

### Shoulder Charge

The ajarakan moves up to half its speed in a straight line. During this move it can move through the spaces of other creatures without provoking opportunity attacks. Each creature the ajarakan moves through must succeed on a DC 17 Dexterity saving throw or take 18 (3d8 + 5) bludgeoning damage and be knocked prone.

### Lava Spray (Costs 2 Actions)

While in the Ignited State, the ajarakan digs its hands into the ground, melting it before spraying it in a 30-foot line that is 10 feet wide. Each creature in that line must make a DC 17 Dexterity saving throw, taking 22 (4d10) fire damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one. 244
