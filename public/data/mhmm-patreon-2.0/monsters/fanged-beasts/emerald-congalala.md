# Emerald Congalala

## Bio

The emerald congalala was a rumored subspecies of congalala. This monster's true identity is a congalala that dyed its hair green in order to impress a conga pack. By dyeing its hair green, emerald congalala can potentially become the next leader of the pack. It is unknown what it uses to dye its hair; however, the substance makes its elemental resistances stronger in some regards.

Emerald congalala are an opportunistic species that will eat just about anything they can get their claws on. The emerald congalala have been observed eating mushrooms, plants, carrion, trees, hermitaur, and other smaller monsters. Compared to normal Congalala, Emerald Congalala are known to be able to release more flatulate gas from their body.

This gas can easily send hunters flying into the air, even knocking out some of them. They are very curious of its surroundings, but also more aggressive.

### Stench

Any creature other than a conga or congalala that starts its turn within 5 feet of the congalala must succeed on a DC 17 Constitution saving throw or be poisoned until the start of the creature's next turn. On a successful saving throw, the creature is immune to the stench of all congalalas for 1 hour.

### Standing Leap

The congalala's long jump is up to 15 feet and its high jump is up to 15 feet, with or without a running start.


## Actions

### Multiattack

The congalala makes three Fist attacks.

### Fist

Melee Weapon Attack: +10 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 15 (2d8 + 6) bludgeoning damage.

### Belly Thrust

Melee Weapon Attack: +10 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 17 (2d10 + 6) bludgeoning damage.

### Deadly Leap

If the congalala jumps at least 15 feet as part of its movement, it can then use this action to land on its feet in a space that contains one or more other creatures. Each of those creatures must succeed on a DC 18 Strength or Dexterity saving throw (target's choice) or be knocked prone and take 33 (6d8 + 6) bludgeoning damage. On a successful save, the creature takes only half the damage, isn't knocked prone, and is pushed 5 feet out of the congalala's space into an unoccupied space of the creature's choice. If no unoccupied space is within range, the creature instead falls prone in the congalala's space.

### Chow Down (Recharge 6)

The congalala eats the food it is holding in its tail before unleashing a breath attack in a 30-foot cone with varying effects. Each creature in that area must make a DC 17 Constitution saving throw, taking 28 (8d6) damage of the food's damage type and suffer any additional effect on a failed save or half as much damage and doesn't suffer from any additional effects on a successful one.


## Reactions

### Belly Thrust

When the congalala is hit by a melee attack, it can use its reaction to make a belly thrust attack against the creature.

### Fart

When the congalala is hit by a melee weapon attack, it can use its reaction to release a noxious fart at the attacker. If the attacker is concentrating on a spell or spell-like ability, it must succeed on a DC 17 Constitution saving throw, to maintain concentration on it.


## Legendary Actions
