## Actions

### Multiattack

The congalala uses Fart if available and then makes two Fist attacks.

### Belly Thrust

Melee Weapon Attack: +9 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 16 (2d10 +5) bludgeoning damage.

### Fist

Melee Weapon Attack: +9 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 14 (2d8 + 5) bludgeoning damage.

### Feces Fling

Ranged Weapon Attack: +9 to hit, range 20/60 ft., one target. Hit: 9 (1d8 + 5) acid damage. If the target is a creature, it emanates a horrid stench for 1 hour or until a creature casts the prestidigitation spell on it or uses an item that removes the odor.

### Deadly Leap

If the congalala jumps at least 10 feet as part of its movement, it can then use this action to land prone on its belly in a space that contains one or more other creatures. Each of those creatures must succeed on a DC 17 Strength or Dexterity saving throw (target's choice) or be knocked prone and take 27 (5d10 + 5) bludgeoning damage. On a successful save, the creature takes only half the damage, isn't knocked prone, and is pushed 5 feet out of the congalala's space into an unoccupied space of the creature's choice. If no unoccupied space is within range, the creature instead falls prone in the congalala's space.

### Fart

If the congalala used Rancid Burp on its previous turn, it can use this action to release a noxious fart in a 10-foot cube originating from it. Each creature in that area must succeed on a DC 17 Constitution saving throw or take 11 (2d10) acid damage, be poisoned until the start of congalala's next turn, and emanate a stench for 1 hour or until a creature casts the prestidigitation spell on it or uses an item that removes the odor.

### Rancid Burp (Recharge 5-6)

If the Congalala has used Chow Down this round or since it last used Rancid Burp, it can eat the mushroom as part of this action, then exhale noxious gas in a 30-foot line that is 10 feet wide. Each creature in that line must make a DC 17 Dexterity or Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, the creature takes 35 (10d6) damage of the mushroom's type and the effect of the mushroom. On a successful save, the creature takes half as much damage and doesn't suffer the effect of the mushroom.


## Reactions

### Belly Thrust

When the congalala is hit by a melee attack, it can use its reaction to make a Belly Thrust attack against the attacker.


## Legendary Actions

The congalala can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action option can be used at a time and only at the end of another creature's turn. The congalala regains spent legendary actions at the start of its turn.

### Fling

The congalala makes on Feces Fling attack.

### Chow Down

The congalala digs up hidden mushroom. Roll a d6 to determine what mushroom it now holds with Carried Mushroom.

### Wild Swipes (Costs 3 Actions)

The congalala moves up to half its speed in a straight line and makes three Fist attacks and falls prone at the end of its movement. 264
