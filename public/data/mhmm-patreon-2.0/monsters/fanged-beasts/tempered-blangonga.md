## Actions

### Multiattack

The blangonga makes three Fist attacks. It can use its Boulder in place of any melee attack.

### Fist

Melee Weapon Attack: +10 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 14 (2d8 + 5) bludgeoning damage. If the blangonga moved at least 10 feet straight toward a target immediately before the hit the target takes an Ice Spray (Recharge 5-6). The blangonga exhales a spray of ice shards in a 30-foot cone. Each creature in that area must make a DC 19 Dexterity saving throw, taking 49 (12d6) cold damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one. If the saving throw fails by 5 or more the creature is restrained by ice. As an action, the restrained target can make a DC 15 Strength check, bursting the ice on a success. The ice can also be attacked and destroyed (AC 14; hp 15; vulnerability to fire damage; immunity to piercing, cold, poison, and psychic damage).


## Legendary Actions

The blangonga can take 2 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action option can be used at a time and only at the end of another creature's turn. The blangonga regains spent legendary actions at the start of its turn.

### Lariat

The blangonga moves up to 10 feet, then makes one Fist attack.

### Retreating Kick (Costs 2 Actions)

The blangonga leaps up to 15 feet away from the nearest hostile creature. During this movement, it can land in a space that contains one or more creatures.
