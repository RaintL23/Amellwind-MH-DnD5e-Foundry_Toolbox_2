# Thunderlord Zinogre

## Bio

The thunderlord zinogre is a legendary fanged wyvern spoken of in Yukumo Village legends, where it is called the Thunder Wolf Wyvern King. This rare variant can store far more lightning than a typical zinogre, allowing it to remain constantly charged. While sharing most traits with its kin, it differs in having one horn larger than the other and golden markings across parts of its body.

Though always charged, the thunderlord zinogre can push beyond a normal zinogre’s limits, entering a “true awakening” state where its lightning turns gold, similar to a rajang’s. In this form, it gains heightened control over its power, and its thunderbugs become supercharged—so much so that even they can barely contain the energy.

High in the food chain, it preys on herbivores such as aptonoth, small bird wyverns, and theropods like kulu-ya-ku, but must still contend with formidable rivals, including Elder Dragons, rathalos, lagiacrus, mizutsune, and glavenus. Known for its unmatched ferocity, the thunderlord zinogre can only be hunted by those granted special permits from the Hunter’s Guild.

## Traits

### Lightning Aura

At the start of each of the zinogre’s turns, each creature within 5 feet of it takes 7 (2d6) lightning damage, and flammable objects in the aura that aren't being worn or carried ignite. A creature that touches the zinogre or hits it with a melee attack while within 5 feet of it takes 7 (2d6) lightning damage.

### Ultracharged State (Recharges after a Short or Long Rest)

When the zinogre is reduced to half of its maximum hit points, it enters an ultracharged state for 1 hour and lets out a booming roar, sending a shockwave out in a 15-foot radius around it. Each creature in that area must succeed on a DC 17 Strength saving throw or take 14 (4d6) thunder damage and be knocked prone.

### Lightning Mines

The zinogre scatters six balls of lightning, each of which lands in an unoccupied space within a 60-foot cone in front of it. The space sparks with electricity until the start of the zinogre's next turn, but the mine remains in the space for 1 hour. When a creature or object enters a space where a mine is located, it explodes, dealing 10 (3d6) lightning damage to the target.

### Lightning Storm (Recharge 5-6)

Numerous bolts of lightning strike the ground in a 60-foot radius around the zinogre. Choose three creatures, each of which must make a DC 17 Dexterity saving throw, taking 38 (7d10) lightning damage and be afflicted with thunderblight for 1 minute on a failed save, or half as much damage and is not afflicted with thunderblight on a successful one. A creature can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the thunderblight on itself on a success.


## Legendary Actions

The zinogre can take 2 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action option can be used at a time and only at the end of another creature's turn. The zinogre regains spent legendary actions at the start of its turn.

### Attack

The zinogre makes one Tail attack.

### Dash Attack (Costs 2 Actions)

The zinogre moves up to its speed without provoking opportunity attacks. Then it makes one Stomp attack.

### Thunderlord's Stomp (Costs 2 Actions)

The zinogre focuses all its energy into one foreleg and makes one stomp attack against a target in range. On a hit, the target takes an extra 21 (6d6) lightning damage.

### Thunderous Somersault (Costs 2 Actions; Ultracharged State Only)

The zinogre executes a somersault while creating lightning bolts with its tail, before landing back in its space. Each creature in a 60-foot line that is 15 feet wide in front of the zinogre must succeed on a DC 17 Dexterity saving throw, taking 27 (6d8) lightning damage, be afflicted with thunderblight for 1 minute, and be knocked prone on a failed save. On a successful save the creature takes half as much damage, isn't afflicted with thunderblight, or knocked prone. 319
