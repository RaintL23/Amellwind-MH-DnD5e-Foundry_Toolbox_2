## Actions

### Multiattack

The great girros makes three Bite attacks.

### Bite

Melee Weapon Attack: +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 11 (2d6 + 4) piercing damage. If the target is a creature, it must make a DC 15 Constitution saving throw, taking 7 (2d6) poison damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one. If the poison damage reduces the target to 0 hit points, the target is stable but poisoned for 1 hour, even after regaining hit points, and is paralyzed while poisoned in this way. If the great girros moved at least 20 feet straight toward a creature immediately before the hit, the target takes an extra 7 (2d6) damage. If the target is a creature, it must succeed on a DC 15 Strength saving throw or be knocked prone.

### Paralyzing Spit

Ranged Weapon Attack: +7 to hit, range 30/120 ft., one creature. Hit: The creature is poisoned for 1 minute. While poisoned in this way the target is paralyzed. A creature can make a DC 15 Constitution saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success.

### Alpha Call (1/day)

The great girros looses a shriek, calling 2 (1d4) girros to its aid. The creatures arrive on initiative 20 (losing initiative ties), acting as allies of the great girros and obeying its hissed commands.
