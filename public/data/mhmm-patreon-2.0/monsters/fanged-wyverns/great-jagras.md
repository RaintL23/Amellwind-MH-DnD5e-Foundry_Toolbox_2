## Traits

### Full Belly

Immediately after the great jagras swallows a creature or object, its belly expands for 1 hour or until it regurgitates the creature or object. While its belly is expanded, its movement speed is reduced by 10 feet and it deals one extra die of damage on Strength-based weapon attacks (included in the attacks).


## Actions

### Multiattack

The great jagras makes one Bite attack and one Claw attack.

### Bite

Melee Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 10 (2d6 + 3) piercing damage or 13 (3d6 + 3) piercing damage if its belly is expanded, and the target is grappled (escape DC 13). Until this grapple ends, the target is restrained, and the great jagras can't bite another target.

### Claw

Melee Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 10 (3d4 + 3) slashing damage or 13 (4d4 + 3) piercing damage if its belly is expanded.

### Swallow

The great jagras makes one Bite attack against a Medium or smaller target it is grappling. If the attack hits, the target is swallowed, and the grapple ends. The swallowed target is blinded and restrained, it has total cover against attacks and other effects outside the great jagras, and it takes 10 (3d6) acid damage at the start of each of the great jagras's turns. The great jagras can have only one target swallowed at a time. If the great jagras takes 15 damage or more on a single turn from the swallowed target, the great jagras must succeed on a DC 15 Constitution saving throw at the end of that turn or regurgitate the target, which falls prone in a space within 5 feet of the great jagras and is no longer blinded or restrained by it. While the great jagras isn't incapacitated, it can regurgitate the target at any time (no action required) in a space within 5 feet of it. If it is a creature, it exits prone. If the great jagras dies, it likewise regurgitates a swallowed target.

### Rollover (Full Belly Only, Recharge 5-6)

The great jagras moves up to half its speed while rolling over. During this move, it can move through other creatures without provoking opportunity attacks. Each creature the great jagras moves through must make a DC 13 Dexterity saving throw, taking 17 (4d6 + 3) bludgeoning damage and be knocked prone on a failed save, or half as much damage, isn't knocked prone, and is pushed 5 feet out of the great jagras's space into an unoccupied space of the creature's choice on a successful save. If no unoccupied space is within range, the creature instead falls prone in the great jagras's space.
