# Fey Nerscylla

## Bio

The fey nerscylla differs from the common form primarily in coloration. Its carapace ranges from deep teal to emerald, and its spines show blue-violet iridescence. Its silk carries a faint green luminescence visible in shade. The fey nerscylla hunts by ambush. It lays layered silk canopies and lure lines, then lunges in short bursts to seize and bind prey.

It drags captures to a lined den. It will feign retreat to draw pursuers beneath drop nets. It makes its home in the fey realms, favoring twilight forests, bramble thickets, misted ravines, fungus groves, and crystal-laced caverns. Nests are anchored high in canopies or under rock overhangs.

Sightings on the material plane occur in shadowed forests, steep ravines, and cave systems similar to those used by common nerscylla. The fey nerscylla is notably stronger than its common form. Its hide shows marked resistance to magic, and the silk is tougher to break. Its Egg sacs hang in guarded clusters, and adults mark territory with cast spines and ribboned strands of silk.

## Traits

### Magic Resistance

The nerscylla has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

### Spider Climb

The nerscylla can climb difficult surfaces, including upside down on ceilings, without needing to make an ability check.

### Standing Leap

The nerscylla's long jump is up to 30 feet and its high jump is up to 15 feet, with or without a running start.

### Web Sense

While in contact with a web, the nerscylla knows the exact location of any other creature in contact with the same web.

### Web Walker

The nerscylla ignores movement restrictions caused by webbing.


## Actions

### Multiattack

The nerscylla makes one Poisonous Maw attack and two Claw attacks.

### Claw

Melee Weapon Attack: +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 10 (2d6 + 3) slashing damage, and the target is grappled (escape DC 13).

### Poisonous Maw

Melee Weapon Attack: +7 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 8 (2d4 + 3) piercing damage plus 13 (3d8) poison damage.

### Stinger

The nerscylla impales a webbed creature within 5 feet of it with its stinger. That creature must make a DC 15 Constitution saving throw, taking 28 (8d6) poison damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one. If the saving throw fails by 5 or more, the target falls unconscious for 1 hour, or until it takes damage or another creature uses an action to shake it awake. If the poison damage reduces the target to 0 hit points, the target is stable but poisoned for 1 hour, even after regaining hit points, and is paralyzed while poisoned in this way.

### Webbing Shot (Recharge 5-6)

Ranged Weapon Attack: +7 to hit, range 30/120 ft. , one Large or smaller creature. Hit: The creature is restrained by webbing. As an action, the restrained creature can make a DC 15 Strength check, escaping from the webbing on a success. The creature is no longer restrained if the webbing is destroyed. The webbing has AC 12, 10 hit points, resistance to bludgeoning damage, and immunity to poison and psychic damage.


## Bonus Actions

### Web Swing

The nerscylla fires a string of webbing as thick as a rope towards an object or terrain that is above it and is within 60 feet of it. It can then use the webbing to pull itself up to 30 feet in the direction of the object or terrain.


## Legendary Actions

The nerscylla can take 1 legendary action, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action option can be used at a time and only at the end of another creature's turn. The nerscylla regains the spent legendary actions at the start of its turn.

### Attack

The nerscylla makes a Poisonous Maw attack.

### Web Cartridge

Roll a d6, on a 5 or 6 the nerscylla's Webbing Shot recharges. 533
