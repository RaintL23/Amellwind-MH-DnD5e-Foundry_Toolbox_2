# Lala Barina

## Bio

The lala barina is a large temnoceran with a compact cephalothorax, six spined walking legs, and a segmented abdomen that ends in a retractable stinger. Dense hairs cover much of the body. The thoracic shell can open to display red, petal-like bristles that resemble a bloom. A folded forelimb pair can extend as clawed grasping appendages.

The head is small with multiple eyes and strong chelicerae. The lala barina moves in short, rhythmic bursts with brief pauses which can appear pirouette-like. It produces scarlet silk from the mouth. The silk binds prey and anchors its rapid lunges. When agitated, the abdominal bloom opens, ejecting petal-like bristles that induce rapid paralysis on contact.

Close contact attacks use the stinger, leg spines, and mandibles. Nests form from layered scarlet silk and accumulate around core lairs. The lala barina occupies humid, densely vegetated forests and shaded ravines. It also occurs in ruin-strewn uplands and deep hollows. Nests line cliffs, trunks, and cavern mouths.

Scattered red silk “florets” often mark active territories.

## Traits

### Spider Climb

The lala barina can climb difficult surfaces, including upside down on ceilings, without needing to make an ability check.

### Web Sense

While in contact with a web, the lala barina knows the exact location of any other creature in contact with the same web.

### Web Walker

The lala barina ignores movement restrictions caused by webbing.


## Actions

### Multiattack

The lala barina uses Petal Scatter (if available). It then makes two Claw attacks.

### Claw

Melee Weapon Attack: +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 6 (1d6 + 3) slashing damage.

### Leg

Melee Weapon Attack: +6 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 7 (1d8 + 3) bludgeoning damage.

### Stinger

The lala barina impales a creature within 15 feet of it with its stinger. That creature must make a DC 14 Dexterity or Constitution saving throw, taking 21 (6d6) poison damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one. If the saving throw fails by 5 or more, the target is poisoned for 1 minute. A poisoned creature can repeat its saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on a success.

### Petal Scatter (Recharge 5-6)

The lala barina spins in place, spreading florets in a 30-foot radius that continues to fall until the start of its next turn. Each creature that ends its turn or moves 15 or more feet in that area while the florets fall must succeed on a DC 14 Dexterity or Constitution saving throw or be paralyzed until the end of its next turn.


## Legendary Actions

The lala barina can take 2 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action option can be used at a time and only at the end of another creature's turn. The lala barina regains spent legendary actions at the start of its turn.

### Attack

The lala barina makes one Leg attack.

### Double Stab (Costs 2 Actions)

The lala barina makes one Stinger attack. At the end of the next creature's turn (if it is not its turn), it makes a second Stinger attack. It can't use another legendary action at the end of the turn where it makes the second attack.

### Petal Dance (Costs 2 Actions)

The lala barina moves up to its speed in a straight line. During this movement, it doesn't provoke opportunity attacks as it pirouettes, scattering florets in its path. Each creature that ends its turn or moves 15 or more feet in lala barina's path while the florets fall must succeed on a DC 14 Dexterity or Constitution saving throw or be paralyzed until the end of its next turn. 527
