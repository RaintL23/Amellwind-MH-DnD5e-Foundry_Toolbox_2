# Magma Almudron

## Bio

Magma Almudron are bluish gray and crimson in color, with a red glow that can be seen through the seams between its shell. It is noticeably smaller than a normal Almudron. Magma Almudron skillfully uses its tail to spread magma along the ground. Magma Almudron can secrete a special liquid which allows it to melt the ground itself.

This allow it to dig into the hot volcanic ground, and by doing so, causes some of its body parts to heat up and soften. It can creates pillars of magma to stand on before it eventually explodes. It can also hold a magma-mud ball with its tail like how the normal Almudron holds a mud ball.

It can use the magma bad like a bludgeon to hit its foes before it explodes the moment it hits the ground

## Traits

### Standing Leap

The almudron's long jump is up to 40 feet and its high jump is up to 20 feet, without a running start. In addition, the almudron does not provoke opportunity attacks while moving with a jump.

### Softened Body

When the almudron emerges from underground, while in volcanic terrain, its AC is reduced by 2 until the end of its next turn.

### Magma Boulder

When the almudron emerges from the ground roll a d6. On a 4-6, three magma boulders appear in random spaces within 20 feet of the almudron. On initiative count 20 (losing all ties) the rocks explode and each creature in a 10-foot radius around a rock must succeed on a DC 19 Dexterity saving throw or take 10 (3d6) fire damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.


## Actions

### Multiattack

The almudron two Claw attacks.

### Claw

Melee Weapon Attack: +11 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 15 (2d6 + 5) slashing damage.

### Tail

Melee Weapon Attack: +11 to hit, reach 15 ft., one target. Hit: 18 (3d8 + 5) bludgeoning damage or 32 (6d8 + 5) bludgeoning plus 14 (4d6) fire damage while holding the magmaball. Once per turn, the almudron can cause one of the following additional effects (choose one or roll a d4): 1-2:

### Knockdown

If the target is a creature it must succeed on a DC 19 Strength saving throw or be pushed 10 feet away and knocked prone. 3-4:

### Magmawave

Magma pools form behind the target and flow across a 30-foot cone. Each creature in that area must make a DC 19 Dexterity saving throw, taking 14 (4d6) fire damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.

### Magma Tail Explosion (Recharge 5-6)

While underground the almudron moves up to its burrow speed and emerges from the ground and slams its tail creating an explosion under it. Each creature in a 15foot-radius sphere of the almudron must make a DC 19 Dexterity saving throw, taking 38 (7d10) fire damage or 55 (10d10) fire damage if the almudron is holding a magmaball on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one. Additionally, if the almudron was holding a magmaball, it is destroyed.

### Magma Drive (Recharges after a Short or Long Rest)

The almudron moves up to doubles its burrow speed along the surface, leaving a magma boulder in a space adjacent to it for every 10 feet it moves. On initiative 20 (losing all ties), the magma boulders explode as described in the Magma Boulder trait.


## Legendary Actions

The almudron can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action option can be used at a time and only at the end of another creature's turn. The almudron regains spent legendary actions at the start of its turn.

### Attack

The almudron makes one Claw attack.

### Jump

The almudron jumps up to 40 feet away.

### Tail Swipe (Costs 2 Actions)

Each creature in a 10-foot line that is 15 feet wide adjacent to the almudron must make a DC 18 Strength saving throw, taking 18 (3d8 + 5) bludgeoning damage and be knocked prone on a failed save, or half as much on a successful one.

### Mold Magma Ball (Costs 3 Actions)

The almudron molds a large magmaball that it holds in its tail. The magmaball can be attacked and destroyed AC 15; hp 40; vulnerability to cold damage; immunity to fire, poison, and psychic damage). While holding the magmaball, the almudron doubles its damage dice on its Tail attack (included in the attack). If the almudron lacks the magma to form the ball, it is able to super heat thee ground with its body to create the magma needed.
