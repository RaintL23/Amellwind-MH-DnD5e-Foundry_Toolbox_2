# Ivory Lagiacrus

## Bio

The Ivory Lagiacrus is a pale subspecies of Lagiacrus with an off-white carapace, blue-tipped horns, and cobalt dorsal spines. The dorsal plates are larger than the typical lagiacrus, the limb scutes are thicker, and the webbing between dorsal spines is reduced. Elder ivory lagiacrus often show darker blue banding on the tail spines and heavier wear on the horn edges; which some believe to be an indication of the ivory lagiacrus changing into a abyssal lagiacrus.

The ivory lagiacrus has adapted itself to be more terrestrial, to avoid direct competition with its blue-scaled cousin. It is a top predator that preys on aptonoth, epioth, and jaggi, and it will scavenge when opportunities arise or targets larger monsters like great jaggi or qurupeco; which it can handle without too much trouble.

This change in diet can bring it into conflict with other large hunters such as rathalos, zinogre, and deviljho. Its size and electrical control allow it to withstand most encounters with these rivals.
