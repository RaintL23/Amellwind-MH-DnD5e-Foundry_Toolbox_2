# Balahara

## Bio

The balahara is a serpentine leviathan with four short limbs and a long, drill-like tail. Its body carries small brownish-gray scales that enlarge and sharpen along the flanks into scutes that aid burrowing. The head is tripartite, with three interlocking jaws and a tongue in each section, and both the upper and lower jaws bear saw-edge teeth.

The mouth interior emits a faint blue glow, and rows of tiny, scallop-like ocelli (simple eyes) line the inner jaws while cranial eyes are absent. The balahara has shark-like gill slits sit at the base of the neck and function as sand vents during digging. The balahara operates as a desert ambush predator that “swims” through sand by driving the tail and flexing the body in a corkscrew motion.

It excavates and maintains quicksand pits to immobilize prey, then erupts from below to seize targets. It can spit mud or expel a viscous black mucus during combat. It climbs and clings to walls or pillars when terrain demands vertical access. The species usually hunts alone but can summon nearby balahara to form a pack that attempts to overwhelm larger adversaries.

It relies on acute hearing and light-sensitive jaw ocelli to track movement in shifting sand, and it defends burrow sites with immediate, persistent aggression. The balahara inhabits arid plains and dune seas, including wind-cut canyons and subsand caverns. It establishes lairs on stable dune crests or bedrock shelves.

Records show its activity increases during sandstorms and hot seasonal winds.

### Multiattack

The balahara makes two Claw attacks.

### Claws

Melee Weapon Attack: +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 6 (1d4 + 4) slashing damage. If the target is a creature, it must make a DC 12 Constitution saving throw or be afflicted with waterblight for 1 minute. The target can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, end the effect on a successful save.

### Constrict

Melee Weapon Attack: +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one Large or larger creature. Hit: 15 (2d10 + 4) bludgeoning damage, and the target is grappled (escape DC 14). Until this grapple ends, the creature is restrained, and the balahara can't constrict another target.

### Tail

Melee Weapon Attack: +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 11 (2d6 + 4) bludgeoning damage. If the target is a creature, it must succeed on a DC 14 Strength saving throw or be knocked prone.

### Tail Charge

The balahara uses its speed to lunge forward with its tail in a 30-foot line that is 10-feet wide. Each creature in that area must succeed on a DC 14 Dexterity saving throw or be knocked prone. If the saving throw fails by 5 or more, the creature also takes 11 (2d6 + 4) bludgeoning damage. If a creature is in a space the balahara ends its movement, it is pushed 5 feet out of the balahara's space into an unoccupied space of the creature's choice. If no unoccupied space is within range, the creature instead falls prone in the balahara's space.

### Mud Spray (Recharge 4-6)

The balahara spews mud in a 30-foot line that is 5 feet wide. Each creature in that line must make a DC 12 Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save, the creature takes 9 (2d8) acid damage and is afflicted with waterblight for 1 minute. On a successful save, the creature takes half as much damage and isn't afflicted with waterblight. The blighted creature can make a DC 12 Constitution saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on a successful save.

### Mud Bomb (Recharges after a Short or Long Rest)

The spews a giant ball of mud at a point within 60 feet of it. Each creature in a 10-foot radius sphere of that point must make a DC 12 Dexterity saving throw. On a successful save, the creature takes half as much damage and isn't afflicted with waterblight.
