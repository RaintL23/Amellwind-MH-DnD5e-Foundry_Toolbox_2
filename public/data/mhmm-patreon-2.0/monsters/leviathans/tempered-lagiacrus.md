# Tempered Lagiacrus

- **Languages:** —

## Traits

### Hold Breath

The lagiacrus can hold its breath for 12 hours.

### Legendary Resistance (3/Day)

If the lagiacrus fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.

### Lightning Supremacy

Lightning damage dealt by the lagiacrus bypasses lightning resistance and deals half damage to creatures that are immune to lightning damage.


## Actions

### Multiattack

The lagiacrus makes one Bite attack and one Tail attack.

### Bite

Melee Weapon Attack: +11 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 18 (3d8 + 5) piercing damage + 7 (2d6) lightning damage.

### Body Slam

Melee Weapon Attack: +11 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 21 (3d10 + 5) bludgeoning damage plus 10 (3d6) lightning damage. If the lagiacrus moved at least 20 feet straight toward the target immediately before the hit, the target takes an extra 16 (3d10) bludgeoning damage. If the target is a creature, it must succeed on a DC 19 Strength saving throw or be pushed up to 10 feet away and knocked prone.

### Tail

Melee Weapon Attack: +11 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 15 (3d6 + 5) bludgeoning damage. If the target is a Large or smaller creature, it must succeed on a DC 19 Strength saving throw or be knocked prone.

### Lightning Shot

Ranged Weapon Attack: +11 to hit, range 60/240 ft., one target. Hit: 14 (4d6) lightning damage.

### Lightning Breath (Recharge 5-6)

The lagiacrus exhales lightning in a 90-foot line that is 10 feet wide. If the lagiacrus' Gathering the Storm trait activated in the last hour, it can instead exhale lightning in a 10-foot line that is 60 feet wide. Each creature in that line must make a DC 18 Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save, the creature takes 60 (11d10) lightning damage and is afflicted with thunderblight for 1 minute. On a successful save, the creature takes half as much damage and isn't afflicted with thunderblight. A creature can make a DC 18 Constitution saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on a success.


## Legendary Actions

The lagiacrus can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action option can be used at a time and only at the end of another creature's turn. The lagiacrus regains spent legendary actions at the start of its turn.

### Attack

The lagiacrus makes two Lightning Shot attacks or it moves up to 10 feet and then makes one Bite attack.

### Lightning Charge (Costs 2 Actions)

The lagiacrus moves up to its speed in a straight line. During this move, it can move through the spaces of other creatures without provoking opportunity attacks. If its Gathering the Storm trait activated in the last hour, it can move up to its speed again, but in a different direction. Each creature the lagiacrus moves through must succeed on a DC 19 Dexterity saving throw or take 18 (3d8 + 5) bludgeoning damage plus 10 (3d6) lightning damage.

### Lightning Discharge (Costs 2 Actions)

The lagiacrus expels lightning from its body in a 15-foot radius around it. If the lagiacrus' Gathering the Storm trait activated in the last hour, it releases the lightning in a 30-foot radius instead. Each creature in that area must make a DC 18 Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save, the creature takes 31 (9d6) lightning damage and is afflicted with thunderblight for 1 minute. On a successful save, the creature takes half as much damage and isn't afflicte3d with thunderblight. A creature can make a DC 18 Constitution saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on a success.


## Mythic Actions

If the lagiacrus's Gathering the Storm trait has activated in the last hour, it can use the option below as an action on its second turn after it began gathering energy.

### Maelstrom (Recharges after a Short or Long Rest)

If the lagiacrus still has temporary hit points at the start of its second turn after it began gathering energy, it unleashes the energy in a 300-foot-radius around it. Each creature in that area must make a DC 18 Dexterity or Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, the creature takes 101 (29d6) lightning damage and is afflicted with thunderblight for 1 hour. On a successful save, the creature takes half as much damage and is afflicted with thunderblight for 1 minute. A creature that succeeded on its saving throw can make a DC 18 Constitution saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on a success. 459
