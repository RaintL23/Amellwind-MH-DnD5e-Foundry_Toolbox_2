# Jin Dahaad

## Bio

The jin dahaad is a gigantic leviathan with four powerful limbs, and extensive metallic plating along the head, neck, torso, and tail. Clustered, jagged horn-plates form a hood where the head meets the neck and small eyes sit recessed beneath facial plates. It has rod-like ridges that line the shoulders, spine, and hips, along with spike-studded forelimbs that end in curved claws.

The plates and ridges function as cooling organs that wick heat and promote rapid frost formation across the limbs and carapace. The jin dahaad is as an apex predator in cold cliff biomes. It shunts heat into its dorsal plates to chill its body and the surrounding air, then weaponizes the resulting frost as breath attacks, sweeping blasts, and temporary ice armor.

It climbs sheer faces by super-cooling its claws to adhere to rock and ice, and it can launch freezing beams that immobilize large targets. When threatened, it concentrates cooling across multiple ridges and releases a large freezing discharge that blankets the area in snow and steam.

The jin dahaad prefers to occupy high, wind-scoured ice cliffs and adjacent permafrost basins, favoring terrain with vertical walls, and persistent subzero temperatures. ## Traits

### Armored Body

The jin dahaad's body is covered in four sets of armored plates. One set covers its claws and back legs. Another protects its tail. A third set is located on its back and cannot be reached from the ground. The final set is on its head, which can only be targeted from the ground on rounds when it makes a Bite attack. Each plate can be attacked and destroyed (AC 20; 20 HP; immunity to poison and psychic damage). For each plate destroyed, the Jin Dahaad loses 5 hit points and its AC is reduced by 1 until it finishes a long rest.

### Legendary Resistance (2/Day)

If the jin dahaad fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.


## Actions

### Multiattack

The jin dahaad makes one Bite or Claw attack and one Kick or Tail attack.

### Bite

Melee Weapon Attack: +12 to hit, reach 15 ft., one target. Hit: 16 (4d8 + 7) piercing damage + 4 (1d8) cold damage. If the target is a creature, it must succeed on a DC 21 Constitution saving throw or be afflicted with iceblight for 1 minute. A creature can repeat its saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on a success.

### Claw

Melee Weapon Attack: +12 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 14 (2d6 + 7) slashing damage plus 7 (2d6) cold damage and each creature within 5 feet of the target take damage equal to the cold damage dealt.

### Kick

Melee Weapon Attack: +12 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 16 (2d8 + 7) bludgeoning damage. If the target is a creature, it must succeed on a DC 20 Strength saving throw or be knocked prone.

### Tail

Melee Weapon Attack: +12 to hit, reach 30 ft., one target. Hit: 29 (4d10 + 7) slashing damage.

### Body Slam

The jin dahaad stands on its hind legs, then slams its body on the ground in its space. Each creature on the ground within 30 feet of the jin dahaad must make a DC 20 Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save, the creature takes 9 (2d10) bludgeoning damage plus 14 (4d6) cold damage, is pushed 10 feet away, and knocked prone. On a successful save, the creature takes half as much damage, isn't pushed away, and isn't knocked prone.

### Frost Breath (Recharge 5-6)

The jin dahaad exhales ice and snow in a 45-foot cone or a 90-foot line that is 10feet wide. Each creature in that area must make a DC 21 Constitution saving throw, taking 36 (8d8) cold damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one. If the saving throw fails by 5 or more the creature is restrained by ice. As an action, the restrained target can make a DC 18 Strength check, bursting the ice on a success. The ice can also be attacked and destroyed (AC 18; hp 20; vulnerability to fire damage; immunity to piercing, cold, poison, and psychic damage).

### Subzero Shockwave (Recharges after a Short or Long Rest)

Wind swirls and walls of ice rise from the ground around the Jin Dahaad, pushing each creature within 5 feet of it 5 feet away as it prepares to unleash a devastating attack. Until the start of its next turn the jin dahaad's movement is 0, it has zero legendary actions, and it has total cover from anything outside of the walls. A creature that starts its turn within 80 feet of the jin dahaad must succeed on a DC 21 Strength saving throw or be knocked prone from the wind pressure. If the saving throw fails by 5 or more, the creature is also pulled 10 feet towards the jin dahaad. At the start of the jin dahaad's next turn, it unleashes the energy it stored in an explosion of ice. Each creature within 80 feet of the jin dahaad must make a DC 21 Constitution saving throw, taking 22 (4d10) piercing damage plus 27 (5d10) cold damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.


## Legendary Actions

The jin dahaad can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action option can be used at a time and only at the end of another creature's turn. The jin dahaad regains spent legendary actions at the start of its turn.

### Attack

The jin dahaad makes one Claw attack or Kick attack.

### Lunging Bite

The jin dahaad moves 10 feet towards a target and makes one Bite attack against it.

### Frost Mist (Costs 2 Actions)

The jin dahaad expels freezing mist from its body. Each creature on the ground within 10 feet of the jin dahaad must succeed on a DC 21 Strength saving throw or be restrained by ice. As an action, the restrained target can make a DC 18 Strength check, bursting the ice on a success. The ice can also be attacked and destroyed (AC 18; HP 20; vulnerability to fire damage; immunity to piercing, cold, poison, and psychic damage).

### Charge (Costs 2 Actions)

The jin dahaad moves up to its speed. During this move, it can move through the spaces of other creatures without provoking opportunity attacks. Each creature the jin dahaad moves through must succeed on a DC 20 Dexterity saving throw or take 20 (3d8 + 7) bludgeoning damage, be pushed 15 feet away and knocked prone.

### Flash Freeze (Costs 3 Actions)

The jin dahaad freezes the ground in a 60-foot radius around it. Each creature in that area must succeed on a DC 21 Strength saving throw or take 14 (4d6) cold damage and be restrained by ice. As an action, the restrained target can make a DC 18 Strength check, bursting the ice on a success. The ice can also be attacked and destroyed (AC 18; HP 20; vulnerability to fire damage; immunity to piercing, cold, poison, and psychic damage). 454
