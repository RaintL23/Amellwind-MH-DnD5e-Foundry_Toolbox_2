# Royal Ludroth

## Bio

The royal ludroth has a crown-like crest composed of several finger-like appendages tipped with black spikes as well as a large, spongy yellow mane. It has a long, serpentine body with four squat limbs. Its hind pair of legs are shaped like paddles for swimming, and its foremost pair are more muscular, tipped with developed digits ending in long black claws.

Like great jaggi, the males of the ludroth species increase in size and develop physical changes that set them apart from younger males and the opposite gender, in this case a large, spongy mane. This mane absorbs water like a sponge, allowing the royal ludroth to survive out of water for some time.

Royal ludroth are rather calm in nature when not disturbed but can be provoked easily. A harem of ludroth are led by only one royal ludroth and the harem usually has only about ten females in it. Most harems aren't very big.

## Actions

### Multiattack

The royal ludroth makes two Claw attacks.

### Claws

Melee Weapon Attack: +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 11 (2d6 + 4) slashing damage. If the target is a creature, it must make a DC 14 Constitution saving throw or be afflicted with waterblight for 1 minute. The target can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, end the effect on a successful save.

### Mucus Spit (Recharge 5-6)

The royal ludroth spits a glob of mucus at point within 60 feet of it. Each creature in a 5-foot radius of that point must make a DC 15 Dexterity saving throw, taking 17 (5d6) acid damage and be afflicted with waterblight for 1 minute on a failed save, or half as much damage and isn't afflicted with waterblight on a successful save. The target can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on a successful save.
