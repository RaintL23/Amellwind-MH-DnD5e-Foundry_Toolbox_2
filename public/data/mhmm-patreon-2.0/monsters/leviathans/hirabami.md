# Hirabami

## Bio

The hirabami is a large leviathan with an elongated body similar to that of the somnacanth. It has a pointed head armored in segmented plates, and a lower jaw that can split along the midline. Small, pale eyes sit high on the face, while broad membranes run from the sides of the head down the long neck to the shoulders.

The forelimbs have fin-like webbing from the elbow to its a sickle-shaped claws; the hind limbs are narrow and pad-ended. The tail widens at the end lined with multiple pairs of clamping claws. The hirabami uses its neck membranes to ride wind currents and remain aloft without flapping.

It can cling to vertical surfaces or hang below arches by gripping with the tail claws or foreclaws, and it rests in groups on sheltered ledges. When threatened or hunting, it projects icy blasts from the mouth and relies on sudden dives and banking turns to catch targets off-guard.

Individuals are frequently encountered in groups of three, and they defend nesting ledges with coordinated passes. The hirabami inhabits cold, wind-swept cliff biomes with sheer rock walls and persistent updrafts, where its gliding membranes are most effective. Official materials associate the species with icy escarpments and high alpine ravines.

## Traits

### False Appearance

If the hirabami is motionless while hanging from the ceiling at the start of combat, it has advantage on its initiative roll. Moreover, if a creature hasn't observed the hirabami move or act, that creature must succeed on a DC 19 Intelligence (Investigation) check to discern that the hirabami isn't ordinary tapestry.

### Ice Walk

The hirabami can move across and climb icy surfaces without needing to make an ability check. Additionally, difficult terrain composed of ice or snow doesn't cost it extra moment.


## Actions

### Multiattack

The hirabami makes two Melee or Ranged attacks.

### Bite

Melee Weapon Attack: +9 to hit, reach 10 ft., two targets. Hit: 14 (2d8 + 5) piercing damage plus 3 (1d6) cold damage. If the hirabami is flying and dives at least 20 feet straight toward a target immediately before the hit, the target takes an extra 9 (2d8) piercing damage.

### Tail

Melee Weapon Attack: +9 to hit, reach 15 ft., one target. Hit: 19 (4d6 + 5) bludgeoning damage. If the target is a Medium or smaller creature it must succeed on a DC 15 Strength saving throw or be pushed 5 feet away and knocked prone.

### Frost Spit

Ranged Weapon Attack: +9 to hit, range 60/240 ft., one target. Hit: 14 (4d6) cold damage. If the target is a creature it is restrained by ice. As an action, the restrained target can make a DC 15 Strength check, bursting the ice on a success. The ice can also be attacked and destroyed (AC 12; hp 10; vulnerability to fire damage; immunity to piercing, cold, poison, and psychic damage).

### Rapid Spin (Recharge 5-6)

The hirabami spins rapidly into the air before smashing down and extending its tail to strike targets near it. Each creature within 25 feet of the hirabami must make a DC 17 Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save, the creature takes 26 (6d6 + 5) bludgeoning damage, is pushed 10 feet away, and knocked prone. On a successful save, the creature takes half as much damage, isn't pushed away, and isn't knocked prone.


## Legendary Actions

The hirabami can take 2 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action option can be used at a time and only at the end of another creature's turn. The hirabami regains spent legendary actions at the start of its turn.

### Swiping Bite

The hirabami moves up to 10 feet, then makes one Bite attack.

### Side Roll (Costs 2 Actions)

The hirabami moves 15 feet sideway while rolling. During this movement, it can enter a space that contains one or more creatures. Each of those creatures must succeed on a DC 17 Dexterity or Strength saving throw (target's choice) or be knocked prone and take 15 (3d6 + 5) bludgeoning damage. On a successful save, the creature takes only half the damage, isn't knocked prone, and is pushed 5 feet out of the hirabami's space into an unoccupied space of the creature's choice. If no unoccupied space is within range, the creature instead falls prone in the hirabami's space. 451
