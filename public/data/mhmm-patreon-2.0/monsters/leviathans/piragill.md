# Piragill

## Bio

The piragill is a small leviathan covered in lustrous scales with sharp, blade-like ridges along its body and fins. It is semi-aquatic and favors forested waterways and wetlands, where it uses speed in the water to outmaneuver targets. Piragill hunt in coordinated packs that encircle prey; individuals are cautious on their own but will lunge in unison once an opening appears.

Approaches that bring intruders too close to the waterline often trigger rapid, slashing rushes from the group.

## Traits

### Amphibious

The piragill can breathe air and water.

### Pack Tactics

The piragill has advantage on an attack roll against a creature if at least one of the piragill's allies is within 5 feet of the creature and the ally isn't incapacitated.


## Actions

### Headbutt

Melee Weapon Attack: +3 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 4 (1d6 + 1) slashing damage. If the piragill moved at least 10 feet straight toward the target immediately before the hit, the target takes an extra 3 (1d6) slashing damage. If the target is a creature, it must succeed on a DC 12 Strength saving throw or be knocked prone.
