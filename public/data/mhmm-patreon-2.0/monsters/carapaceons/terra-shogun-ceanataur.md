# Terra Shogun Ceanataur

## Bio

While considered a subspecies by the Guild, the terra shogun ceanataur is actually the same species as shogun ceanataur. The terra shogun ceanataurs are particularly aggressive shogun ceanataurs that have moved to harsher environments, such as volcanoes and swamps, to live in solitude.

These environments provide unique sources of nutrition, giving terra shogun ceanataurs a reddish-brown coloration. Living in these harsh environments has also toughened the shells of terra shogun ceanataurs, making them much harder than the average shogun ceanataur. Their habitat also gives them access to the notably tougher skulls of Black Gravios, giving them even more defense against intruders.

Terra shogun ceanataur are quite high in the food chain, feeding on corpses and smaller creatures. Though quite high in the food chain, terra shogun ceanataur are known to get in aggressive battles with other shogun ceanataur. Far more aggressive and offensive than a normal shogun ceanataur, terra shogun ceanataur are known to go through great lengths in order to kill a foe quickly and effectively.

## Traits

### Amphibious

The ceanataur can breathe air and water.

### Black Gravios Skull

The ceanataur protects its vulnerable back with the skull of a black gravios. The skull can be attack and broken the first time its HP is reduced to 0 and destroyed the second time (AC 22, 30 HP; immunity to poison and psychic damage, resistances to piercing, and slashing from nonmagical weapons, vulnerable to bludgeoning damage). Damaging the shell deals no damage to the ceanataur. Once broken, the ceanataur's AC is reduced by 4. Once Destroyed, it is vulnerable to bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage if at least two hostile creatures are within 5 feet of it, and it can't use its Hydropump legendary action.

### Honed Claws

When the ceanataur rolls a 1 or 2 on a damage die for a Claw attack it can reroll the die and must use the new roll, even if the new roll is a 1 or a 2.

### Spider Climb

The ceanataur can climb difficult surfaces, including upside down on ceilings, without needing to make an ability check.


## Actions

### Multiattack

The ceanataur makes two Claw attacks. It can replace one of these attack with its Impaling Strike.

### Claws

Melee Weapon Attack: +11 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 21 (3d10 + 5) slashing damage. If the target is a creature other than an undead or a construct, it must succeed on a DC 19 Constitution saving throw or lose 10 (3d6) hit points at the start of each of its turns due to a bloody wound. Each time the ceanataur hits the wounded target with this attack, the damage dealt by the wound increases by 10 (3d6). Any creature can take an action to stanch the wound with a successful DC 19 Wisdom (Medicine) check. The wound also closes if the target receives magical healing. If the slashing damage reduces the creature to 0 hit points, the ceanataur cuts off one of the target's head. The creature dies if it can't survive without the lost head.

### Impaling Strike

The ceanataur makes a strong downward strike with one of its claws at a creature within 5 feet of it. That creature must make a DC 19 Dexterity saving throw, taking 21 (3d10 + 5) piercing damage and be impaled on the ceanataur’s claw on a failed save, or half as much damage and isn't impaled on a successful one. While impaled the target is grappled (escape DC 14) and loses 10 (3d6) hit points each time the ceanataur makes a Claw attack.


## Bonus Actions

### Scuttle

The ceanataur moves up to its speed in a straight line.


## Legendary Actions

The ceanataur can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action option can be used at a time and only at the end of another creature's turn. The ceanataur regains spent legendary actions at the start of its turn.

### Dig

The ceanataur burrows underground and moves up to half its burrow speed.

### Sweeping Strike (Costs 2 Actions)

The ceanataur sweeps its claws in a 10-foot line that is 35 feet wide. Each creature in the area must make a DC 19 Dexterity saving throw, taking 22 (4d10) slashing damage and increases the damage from its wound by 10 (3d6) on a failed save. On a successful one, the target takes half damage and does not increase the bloody wounds damage.

### Hydropump (Costs 3 Actions)

While its shell is still intact, the ceanataur releases a high pressure stream of water from its shell across a 60-foot cone. Each creature in that area must make a DC 20 Dexterity saving throw, taking 44 (8d10) acid damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one. 88
