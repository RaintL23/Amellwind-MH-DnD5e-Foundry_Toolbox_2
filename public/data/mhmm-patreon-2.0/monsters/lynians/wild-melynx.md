# Wild Melynx

## Stat Block

*Small humanoid (lynian), unaligned*
- **Armor Class:** 13 (hide)
- **Hit Points:** 28 (8d6)
- **Speed:** 25 ft., burrow 10 ft.
- **Senses:** passive Perception 10
- **Languages:** Common, Lynian
- **Proficiency Bonus:** +2
- **Challenge:** 1/8

## Traits

### Loot

A wild melynx can hold one stolen item at a time. When it uses Snatch, it will prioritize herbs first, potions second, or any other item of the DMs choice third. It cannot steal anything that weighs more than 9 lbs.

### Pilfer

When the wild melynx steals an item, it will use its movement to flee from the target as if it was frightened. Once it is 60 feet away from all enemies, it will burrow underground and travel to its "base."


## Actions

### Multiattack

The wild melynx makes two Catspaw Mace attacks.

### Catspaw Mace

Melee Weapon Attack: +1 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 2 (1d6 - 1) bludgeoning damage.

### Snatch

The wild melynx attempts to steal an object from its target. The target must make a DC 11 Dexterity saving throw or have an item stolen. A wild melynx drops the stolen item if it takes 5 damage on a single turn or when it dies. 607
