# Porkeplume

## Bio

The porkeplume is a small, bipedal bird wyvern with a fan of long, feathered bristles that rise from its neck and tail. It frequents open scrub and broken grasslands, where it roots for bulbs, grubs, and fallen fruit with a shovel snout and stiff foreclaws. Groups drift while foraging and keep contact with soft chirps.

When threatened, they will puff out the bristles on their head, and a member of the herd may charge at the enemy while the others flee.
