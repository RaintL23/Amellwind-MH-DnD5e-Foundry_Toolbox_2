The gypceros makes three Peck attacks.

Melee Weapon Attack: +9 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 8 (1d8 + 4) piercing damage plus 7 (2d6) poison damage.

### Melee Weapon Attack

+9 to hit, reach 20 ft., one target. Hit: 13 (2d8 + 4) bludgeoning damage.

### Poison Spit

The gypceros spits poison mucus at a point within 120 feet of it. Each creature in a 5-foot radius sphere centered on that point must make a DC 19 Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save, the creature takes 21 (6d6) poison damage and is poisoned for 1 minute. On a successful save, the creature takes half as much damage and isn't poisoned. The poisoned creature can make a DC 19 Constitution saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success.

### Flash (Recharge 5-6)

The gypceros's lamp-like crest on top of its head creates a blinding flash of white light in a 60-foot radius around it. Each creature in that area must succeed on a DC 19 Constitution saving throw or be blinded for 1 minute. The target can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success.

### Thrash

If the gypceros is playing dead, it can then use this action to strike each creature in a 10-foot radius around it. Each creature in that area must make a DC 17 Dexterity saving throw, taking 35 (10d6) and be knocked prone on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one and isn't knocked prone.


## Reactions

### Play Dead (Recharges after a Short or Long Rest)

When the gypceros takes damage while below half of its maximum hit points, it can use this reaction to play dead for up to 1 hour. While playing dead, the gypceros is blinded, prone, and appears dead to all nonmagical outward inspections unless a creature succeeds on a DC 22 Intelligence (Investigation) check.


## Legendary Actions

The gypceros can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action option can be used at a time and only at the end of another creature's turn. The gypceros regains spent legendary actions at the start of its turn.

### Lunging Peck

The gypceros moves 5 feet. It then makes one Peck attack.

### Tail

The gypceros makes one Tail attack.

### Poison Charge (Costs 2 Actions)

The gypceros moves up to its speed without provoking opportunity attacks. During this move, it can move through the spaces of other creatures. Each creature the gypceros moves through must make a DC 17 Dexterity saving throw, taking 17 (3d8 + 4) bludgeoning damage and be knocked prone on a failed save, or half as much damage and isn't knocked prone on a successful one. Additionally, as it moves, the gypceros spits poison mucus in adjacent spaces on both side of its path. Each creature in this area must make a DC 19 Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, the creature takes 10 (3d6) poison damage and is poisoned for 1 minute. On a successful save, the creature takes half as much damage and isn't poisoned. The poisoned creature can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success.
