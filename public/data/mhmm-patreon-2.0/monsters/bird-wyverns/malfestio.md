# Malfestio

## Bio

Malfestio is a bird wyvern that resembles an owl. Its body is covered in blue plumage with a grayish belly, a yellow collar, and a white face with a small beak and red eyes. malfestio has long head tufts tipped with yellow, and its legs have yellow markings and end in scaly feet with two talons and a small vestigial toe.

Its wings have bright blue membranes and long, blade-like claws along the edges. It has a flattened tail that ends in a three-pointed shape. Malfestio are a nocturnal predator that uses its talons and wing claws to attack prey. Like nargacuga, it sleeps in trees to avoid terrestrial predators.

It can produce an ultrasound beam that can easily confuse predator and prey. It has claws along its wings that are usually hidden but are revealed once the malfestio feels threatened or enraged. Due to the way malfestio's eyes are positioned, it will rotate and maneuver its head to allow it to see better.

### Wings

Melee Weapon Attack: +9 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 16 (2d10 + 5) slashing damage.

### Sonic Wave (Recharge 5-6)

The malfestio releases a sonic wave in a 30-foot cone. Each creature in that area must succeed a DC 15 Wisdom saving throw or be confused for 1 minute. While confused, the creature can't take reactions until the start of its next turn and rolls a d8 to determine what it does during that turn. On a 1 to 4, the creature does nothing. On a 5 or 6, the creature takes no action but uses all its movement to move in a random direction. On a 7 or 8, the creature makes one melee attack against a random creature, or it does nothing if no creature is within reach. A creature can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success. If the saving throw fails by 5 or more, the creature instead falls unconscious for 1 minute, until the sleeper takes damage, or until a creature uses an action to shake or slap the sleeper awake. When a creature wakes up it is still confused until it succeeds on its saving throw or 1 minute has passed since it first fell asleep.
