# Hypnocatrice

## Bio

Hypnocatrice is a large-sized bird wyvern covered in bright, colorful feathers, and strong legs tipped with large feet it uses to kick its prey. Unlike other bird wyverns, hypnocatrice does not have fangs or grinding teeth. Instead, it swallows pebbles and soil to help break down its food, forming a prized hypno bezoar stone inside its stomach over time.

Even though the it is covered in feathers, the hypnocatrice flies using bat-like, membraned wings, and its feathers are mainly used for display and attracting mates. Hypnocatrice are typically found in lush environments such as forests and jungles but have also been encountered in harsh deserts.

Their feathers change color during mating season, shifting from rusty orange to bright red and blue to attract a mate. Some rare hypnocatrice have reddish-pink feathers, red-tinted beaks, and larger head crests, and are known for their surprising strength and aggressive dances. While hypnocatrice usually prefers to avoid combat, it will use its sleep sac, to produce a colorless sleep-inducing mist or powder on adventurer and monster alike knocking them unconscious.

Once asleep, it will use its powerful legs to deliver strong kicks.

## Actions

### Multiattack

The hypnocatrice makes two Talon attacks and one Peck attack.

### Talons

Melee Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 10 (2d6 + 3) slashing damage.

### Peck

Melee Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 12 (2d8 + 3) piercing damage.

### Sleep Gas (Recharge 5-6)

The hypnocatrice spits out a bubble of sleeping gas at a location within 30 feet of it. Upon impact, the bubble bursts releasing sleep gas in a 5-foot radius around it. Each creature in that area must make a DC 12 Constitution saving throw or fall unconscious for 1 minute, until the sleeper takes damage, or until a creature uses an action to shake or slap the sleeper awake.

### Wing Attack (3/day)

The hypnocatrice beats its wings. Each creature within 10 feet of the hypnocatrice must succeed on a DC 13 Dexterity saving throw or take 10 (2d6 + 3) bludgeoning damage and be knocked prone. The hypnocatrice can then fly up to half its flying speed. 21
