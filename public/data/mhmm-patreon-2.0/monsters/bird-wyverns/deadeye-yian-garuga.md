# Deadeye Yian Garuga

## Bio

True to its name, this battle-scarred yian garuga only possesses one glowing eye, that leaves a red trail when it moves, similar to a nargacuga. Its body is covered in scars from many fights, and one ear is clipped. Deadeye yian garuga produces a fast-acting deadly poison that causes intense pain and quickly incapacitates prey.

Deadeye yian garuga are more aggressive and experienced in combat than the typical yian garuga. It often hunts at night, using its powerful roar and deadly poison to defeat both monsters and hunters. This rare bird wyvern is highly dangerous, having fought and killed many hunters over its lifetime, and is considered one of the most challenging prey for those seeking a true test of skill.

### Sensitive Ear

If the yian garuga takes thunder damage or a thunder spell is used within 60 feet of it, it must succeed on a DC 18 Constitution saving throw or become stunned until the start of its next turn.

### Sickening Fury

The yian garuga has advantage on melee attack rolls against any creature that is poisoned.

## Legendary Actions

The yian garuga can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action option can be used at a time and only at the end of another creature's turn. The yian garuga regains spent legendary actions at the start of its turn.

### Move

The yian garuga moves up to its speed without provoking opportunity attacks.

### Tail Flip

Choose a creature within 10 feet of the yian garuga. That creature must make a DC 20 Constitution saving throw or be poisoned for 1 minute. A creature can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success.

### Wing Attack (Costs 2 Actions)

The yian garuga beats its wings. Each creature within 10 feet of the yian garuga must succeed on a DC 19 Dexterity saving throw or take 14 (2d6 + 7) bludgeoning damage and be knocked prone. The yian garuga can then fly up to half its flying speed.
